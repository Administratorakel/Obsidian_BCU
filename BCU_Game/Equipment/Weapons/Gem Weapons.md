[[Weapon Tiers]]

Gem Weapons

Special rare weapons

They exist separately from normal weapons

Example:

Lv 30 Garnet Slab
Lv 50 Refined Garnet Slab
Lv 75 Awakened Garnet Slab
Lv 100 Resonant Garnet Slab

Evolution:

- Weapon can evolve through levels
- At each evolution: choose Average DMG% or Skill DMG%
- One of two very rare resources needed to evolve

Gem Weapons are:

- Rare
- Identity Weapons
- Long-term investments

There are still normal weapons in the respective levels.

## Gem Weapons
- Drop with **two special randomly rolled stats**: working abbreviations **DSS** and **FKS** (to be renamed).
- **DSS:** Percentage bonus to normal/average attack damage.
- **FKS:** Percentage bonus to ability/skill damage.
- Rolls can lean **DSS-focused**, **FKS-focused**, or fall between; exact ranges/distribution are undecided.
- Whether these two stats can be improved after drop is **undecided**.
- This is separate from up to four ordinary random bonus attributes unless later rules say otherwise.
