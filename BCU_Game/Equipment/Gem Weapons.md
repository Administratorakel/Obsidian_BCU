Gem Weapons

Special rare weapons

They exist separately from normal weapons

Example:

Lv 30 Garnet Slab

Lv 50 Refined Garnet Slab

Lv 75 Awakened Garnet Slab

Lv 100 Resonant Garnet Slab

Evolution:

- Weapon can evolve through levels
    
- At each evolution: choose Average DMG% or Skill DMG%
    
- One of two very rare resources needed to evolve
    

Gem Weapons are:

- Rare
    
- Identity Weapons
    
- Long-term investments
    

There are still normal weapons in the respective levels.
