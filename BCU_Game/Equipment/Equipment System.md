## Equipment enhancement and requirements
- **All equipment** can be upgraded from `+0` to `+10`.
- Primarily gated by **Player Level**.
- Some exceptional pieces may also require attribute thresholds, **possibly Gem Weapons**. Provisional.
- Item comparisons should appear even for pieces the player cannot currently equip.
- Items have baseline visible stats and may receive rare bonus attributes.

## Random drop bonuses — sequential model
- On equipment drop, attempt a **5%** roll for bonus #1.
- Only on success, attempt bonus #2 at **5%**, then #3, then #4.
- **Stop at the first failed roll.** Maximum four bonuses.
- 5% per step is an **initial balancing value**.
