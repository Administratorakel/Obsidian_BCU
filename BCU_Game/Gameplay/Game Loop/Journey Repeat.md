### Journey Repeat system

**First journey:**

- Automatic stats are applied.
- Player also gets manual stat points.

**[Stat Resetter]**

- Resets the player's chosen/manual stat allocation.
- Does **not** reset automatic stats.

**[Echoed Stat Resetter]**

- Resets chosen/manual stats **and automatic stats**.
- Requires various rare resources to craft.

**After the first Journey Repeat:**

- The player can eventually have **no automatic stats at all**.
- This gives the player full control over stat allocation after each Journey Repeat.

4 stats per level. 2 automatic and 2 manually chosen.

# Journey Repeat

**Status:** Core reset and retention rules confirmed; numerical benefits TBD.  
**Related:** [[Attributes]], [[Equipment System]], [[Weapon Mastery]], 
## Core loop
- Journey Repeat resets **Player Level to 1**.
- On the first journey, 2 points per level are automatically allocated and 2 are chosen manually.
- After the first Journey Repeat, players gain **full manual control over all 4 level-up attribute points**.
- Players select a **permanent Journey bonus** when repeating. The actual bonus choices have **not** been defined; the older EXP/damage/crit/HP list is outdated as a proposed final set.
- **Weapon Mastery does not reset with Journey Repeat.** Individual weapon masteries can instead be reset through their own separate sacrifice mechanic.

## Level-scaling equipment
- Immediately **before** Journey Repeat, the player may designate **all currently equipped items (up to 8 slots)** as level-scaling equipment.
- Designated items remain usable as the player's level resets and their performance scales with the player's current level.
- Scaling persists on those selected items through later Journey Repeats (**current decision**).
- The selected scaling equipment is **not tradable for now**.
- New and other ordinary equipment does **not** automatically inherit the scaling property.
- The player can continue to equip other items as normal.
- Exact stat-scaling curves and handling of equipment swaps are undecided.
- A possible future change: limit the number of scaling items and permit trading. **Not current.**

## Stat reset items
- `[Stat Resetter]`: placeholder item to reset the player's manually assigned stat points.
- `[Echoed Stat Resetter]`: placeholder rare-resource-crafted item to reset manual **and** first-journey automatic stat allocation.
- Both items remain planned **for now**; their names, recipe and availability are undecided.
