## Base movement
- **Running** is the default movement speed.
- **Shift toggles walking** (current control plan).
- **No sprinting, stamina, or jumping** in the intended design.
- Dash replaces the jumping-oriented mobility role.
- The Unity prototype's previous jump/sprint logic is implementation scaffolding, **not** a final design requirement.
