### Core Stats

|Stat|Current effects|
|---|---|
|**VIT**|HP + Defense|
|**STR**|Physical Damage + Physical Defense|
|**INS**|Magical Damage + Magical Defense + reduces **Ability Stability costs**|
|**AGI**|Attack Speed + Movement Speed + Crit Chance + Crit Damage|

### Stat scaling

- **VIT:** HP bonus increases with level. Current idea: **+5 HP per player level** to the VIT HP contribution.
- **STR:** Fixed physical-damage increase per point.
- **INS:** Fixed magical-damage increase per point.
- **AGI:** Small fixed increase to Crit per point.
- **AGI Movement Speed:** Fixed increase per point.
- **No hard stat caps for now.**

### Defense

- Defense currently combines:
    - **Flat damage reduction**
    - **Percentage damage reduction**
- Higher Defense may have **diminishing returns**.
- Exact strength of diminishing returns is still undecided because the numbers need testing.

### Critical Hits

- Every player starts with:
    - **4% Crit Chance**
    - **1.2× Crit Damage**
- Crit Chance and Crit Damage are **separate stats**.
- AGI increases both.
- AGI's Crit scaling should currently be **small per point**.

### Attack Speed

Attack Speed affects **all three**:

- Attack animation time
- Attacks per second
- Recovery time

### Movement Speed

AGI-derived Movement Speed affects:

- Normal running
- Click-to-move
- Attack movement
- **Dash speed**

### Stat Allocation

- Players receive **automatic + manual stats** initially.
- Manual stat points can be allocated **at any time**.
- Stat resets normally require a **specific item or NPC**.
- Automatic stats are fixed rather than build-dependent.

### Journey Repeat system

**First journey:**

- Automatic stats are applied.
- Player also gets manual stat points.

**[Stat Resetter]**

- Resets the player's chosen/manual stat allocation.
- Does **not** reset automatic stats.

**[Echoed Stat Resetter]**

- Resets chosen/manual stats **and automatic stats**.
- Requires various rare resources to craft.

**After the first Journey Repeat:**

- The player can eventually have **no automatic stats at all**.
- This gives the player full control over stat allocation after each Journey Repeat.
