Player & Weapon Progression

- Player Max Level: 100
    
- Weapon progression and unlock pacing are intentionally long-term.
    

Core Rule (refined from your note)

At each Player Level milestone, weapons unlock features tied to approximately 50% of the player’s current level, allowing early preview of later mechanics at reduced effectiveness.

Clean interpretation (design-safe version):

- Players gradually unlock future weapon mechanics early
    
- But full effectiveness only stabilizes later
    
- This creates:
    

- long-term progression
    
- anticipation-based mastery
    
- “learning before power” design

6. PROGRESSION SYSTEM

🧍 Player Level System

- Max Level: 100
    
- Long-term progression is intentional
    

****

Weapon unlocks scale at ~50% of player level

Meaning:

- players see future mechanics early
    
- mastery takes time to fully unlock
    
- progression feels anticipatory, not instant
    

---

🗡️ Weapon Progression Structure

Each weapon includes:

- Stats scaling
    
- Mod slots
    
- Combo variants
    
- Bulletjump upgrades
    
- Augments
    


**

Design Philosophy:

- Progression = mastery of movement + timing
    
- Not just numerical power increase
    

**
Example: Twin Daggers

Lv 1–9

- Base mechanics
    
- Basic Bulletjump
    

Lv 10

- Combo unlock: Razor Dance
    

Lv 20

- Bulletjump Augment: Blink Step
    

Lv 30

- High mastery bonuses
    
- Synergy traits unlocked
