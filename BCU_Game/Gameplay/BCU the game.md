Identity:
**

A cooperative action RPG where players fight instability, develop personal combat philosophies, build legendary equipment, and permanently contribute to the evolution of a living world.

The strongest recurring themes are:

- Combat → Mastery
    
- Equipment → Legacy
    
- World → Stabilization
    
- Story → Understanding Certainty and Uncertainty
