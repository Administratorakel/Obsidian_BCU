## Detection
- Enemy-dependent detection methods
- Proximity
- Line of sight
- Scent tracking
- [[Intelligence Tiers]] affects decision-making
- 🔮 Sound detection deferred

## Losing Sight
- Basic enemies:
    - Move toward last known position
    - Disengage if target cannot be found
- Investigative enemies:
    - Investigate last known position
    - Search nearby locations
- Scent-tracking enemies:
    - Can follow targets without line of sight
    - [[Wolves]] use scent tracking

## Unreachable Targets
- Enemy-dependent behavior
- Basic enemies:
    - Wait or disengage
- Intelligent enemies:
    - Search for alternative routes
- All enemies respect leash boundaries

## Enemy Memory
- Early enemies reset hostility after disengaging
- Advanced enemies may remember attackers temporarily
