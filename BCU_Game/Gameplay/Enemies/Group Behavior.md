## Group Aggression
- Established groups share aggression
- Attacking one member can alert the group
- Group members may use different tactics

## Communication
- Automatic group aggro
- Deliberate alerts
- Calls for assistance
- Coordinated tactics
- Communication abilities depend on enemy variants

## Coordination
- Basic groups:
    - Shared aggression
    - Independent attacks
- Advanced groups:
    - Flanking
    - Surrounding targets
    - Coordinated attacks
- [[Intelligence Tiers]] influences complexity

## Reinforcements
- Some enemies can summon additional enemies
- Summoning is independent of ordinary group aggro
- [[Elite Wolves]] can use reinforcement mechanics

## Respawning
- Entire group must be defeated before respawning
- Faster group clears shorten respawn cooldown
- Slower clears increase cooldown
- Longer respawn pause after repeated group clears
- Exact values depend on map and group
