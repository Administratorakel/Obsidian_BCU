### Player movement

- Running speed = 10
    
- Walking speed = 5
    
- Walking is toggleable
    
- Instant acceleration/deceleration
    
- Instant 180° direction changes
    
- No sprint
    
- No stamina
    
- No jumping
    
- Movement speed can be modified
    
- Walk toggle = “Ctrl + Shift”
    
- Movement modifiers stack additively and multiplicatively
