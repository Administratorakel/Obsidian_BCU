# Shattering Voltage — Status Combination

**Identity:** Frozen-target burst with Lightning spread and control  
**Combination:** [[Ice]] + [[Lightning]]  
**Category:** Elemental + Elemental  
**Trigger:** 6 Ice + 6 Lightning stacks  
**Consumption:** Both contributing sets are consumed on the original target

## Core Mechanics

- The primary enemy's frozen body fractures under electrical discharge, dealing **heavy burst damage**.
- The primary enemy receives an additional stun, **provisionally 1 second**.
- Nearby enemies take damage from the explosion.
- Each nearby affected enemy receives **3 Lightning stacks**, causing the existing **0.5-second Lightning stun** upon reaching the three-stack threshold.
- No Ice stacks are spread.

## Timing and Status Interactions

- The Lightning stacks applied by the explosion count as **real status applications** and can contribute to subsequent combinations if they complete another target's required stacks.
- A pre-existing Lightning Discharge party buff continues for its original duration, even when Lightning stacks are consumed by this combination.

## Undecided

- Primary and area damage, blast radius, primary target stun duration (currently provisional), and interactions with enemy control resistance.

## Shared System Rules

## Activation and Ownership

- The system checks for an eligible combination when a compatible status reaches its full-stack threshold, **before** resolving that status's standalone sixth-stack payoff.
- Only the player's own stacks and eligible **allied** players' stacks can contribute. Non-allied players cannot combine stacks with one another.
- Activation is automatic when the requirements are satisfied; it does not need a separate button press.
- When two allied players supply a combination, damage credit is split **50/50** between them.
- If multiple combinations compete for the same stacks, the first eligible pair to complete resolves first. Truly simultaneous event ordering has not been finalized.
