### Cryotoxicity

**Activation:** Intended as a maximum-stack Ice + Poison interaction; detailed triggering remains to be finalized.

- Poison damage is temporarily suspended while the enemy is frozen.
- Damage that would normally occur during Freeze is accumulated.
- When the enemy thaws, the stored Poison damage is released immediately as one burst.
- **No additional damage multiplier** is applied.

**Example:**

If Poison would deal 1,000 damage per second and the enemy remains frozen for 3 seconds:

- Damage stored: 3,000.
- Damage released when thawing: 3,000.
- Bonus damage: 0.

**Combat identity:** Damage timing and delayed burst.

**Undecided:** Stack consumption, interaction with Poison expiration, and exact activation conditions.
