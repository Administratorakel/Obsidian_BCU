# Conductive Wounds — Status Combination

**Identity:** Repeated-attack payoff through electrically charged wounds  
**Combination:** [[Lightning]] + [[Slash]]  
**Category:** Elemental + Physical  
**Trigger:** 6 Lightning + 6 Slash stacks  
**Consumption:** Both contributing completed sets are consumed

## Core Mechanics

- The target's wounds become electrically charged.
- Further attacks against that target trigger additional Lightning strikes.
- Strikes **may chain** to nearby enemies.

## Timing and Status Interactions

- Consuming Lightning for this combination does not cancel any Lightning Discharge party buff that was already granted; the buff continues for its original duration unless superseded by a new Discharge buff.
- This combination requires **follow-up hits** to receive its payoff.

## Undecided

- Duration, strike frequency, damage values, target selection, chain range and count.
- Whether additional strikes apply Lightning status stacks.

## Shared System Rules

## Activation and Ownership

- The system checks for an eligible combination when a compatible status reaches its full-stack threshold, **before** resolving that status's standalone sixth-stack payoff.
- Only the player's own stacks and eligible **allied** players' stacks can contribute. Non-allied players cannot combine stacks with one another.
- Activation is automatic when the requirements are satisfied; it does not need a separate button press.
- When two allied players supply a combination, damage credit is split **50/50** between them.
- If multiple combinations compete for the same stacks, the first eligible pair to complete resolves first. Truly simultaneous event ordering has not been finalized.
