### Conductive Wounds

**Activation:** 6 Lightning + 6 Slash stacks.

Both statuses are consumed.

- The enemy's wounds become electrically charged.
- Subsequent attacks against the affected enemy trigger additional Lightning strikes.
- Lightning may chain toward nearby enemies.

**Combat identity:** Rewards repeated attacks against an electrically wounded enemy.

**Undecided:** Duration, additional Lightning damage, strike frequency, chain targeting, maximum targets, and whether chains apply Lightning stacks.
