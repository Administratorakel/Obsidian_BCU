### Shattering Voltage

**Activation:** 6 Ice + 6 Lightning stacks.

Both statuses are consumed on the original enemy.

**Main target**

- Frozen ice violently fractures under electrical discharge.
- The target receives powerful burst damage.
- The target receives an additional stun, provisionally 1 second.

**Surrounding enemies**

- Take damage from the explosion.
- Receive **3 Lightning stacks**.
- The existing Lightning stack rule causes a **0.5-second stun**

No Ice stacks are distributed.

**Combat identity:** Powerful single-target burst combined with area damage and crowd control.

**Undecided:** Exact burst damage, area damage, radius, and balancing.
