# Cool Combustion — Status Combination

**Identity:** Enlarged Combustion against a frozen target  
**Combination:** [[Fire]] + [[Ice]]  
**Category:** Elemental + Elemental  
**Trigger:** Fire's Combustion occurs while the target is **Frozen**  
**Consumption:** Fire is consumed by Combustion; any additional Ice consumption is undecided

## Core Mechanics

- Combustion occurring on a frozen enemy gains an **increased blast range** around that enemy.
- The vault specifies **100% of Combustion's damage** across the expanded area.

## Timing and Status Interactions

- An ordinary Freeze lasts **3 seconds**; further Ice hits may extend it by **1 second once per source**.
- This interaction uses the **active Freeze window** rather than requiring Fire to hold an otherwise consumed six-stack set.
- Fire's existing Combustion mechanics and Burn application remain referenced from [[Fire]].

## Undecided

- Exact radius increase and any further Ice stack consumption.

## Shared System Rules

## Activation and Ownership

- The system checks for an eligible combination when a compatible status reaches its full-stack threshold, **before** resolving that status's standalone sixth-stack payoff.
- Only the player's own stacks and eligible **allied** players' stacks can contribute. Non-allied players cannot combine stacks with one another.
- Activation is automatic when the requirements are satisfied; it does not need a separate button press.
- When two allied players supply a combination, damage credit is split **50/50** between them.
- If multiple combinations compete for the same stacks, the first eligible pair to complete resolves first. Truly simultaneous event ordering has not been finalized.
