# Cryotoxicity — Status Combination

**Identity:** Stored Poison damage released when Freeze ends  
**Combination:** [[Ice]] + [[Poison]]  
**Category:** Elemental + Elemental  
**Trigger:** Maximum-stack Ice + Poison interaction; exact timing needs confirmation  
**Consumption:** Undecided

## Core Mechanics

- While the enemy is frozen, Poison's normal damage over time is **suspended**.
- The Poison damage that would have occurred during Freeze is **stored**.
- When Freeze ends, all stored damage is dealt in **one burst**.
- No bonus multiplier is applied to the stored damage.

## Example

If Poison would deal 1,000 damage per second during a 3-second Freeze, Cryotoxicity stores 3,000 damage and releases 3,000 when the enemy thaws.

## Timing and Status Interactions

- Ordinary Freeze starts at **3 seconds** and can be extended by **1 second once per Ice source**.
- Cryotoxicity changes **when** Poison's damage occurs, not its established total damage.

## Undecided

- Precise trigger conditions, consumption of source stacks, behavior when Poison expires while frozen, and handling of early Freeze removal.

## Shared System Rules

## Activation and Ownership

- The system checks for an eligible combination when a compatible status reaches its full-stack threshold, **before** resolving that status's standalone sixth-stack payoff.
- Only the player's own stacks and eligible **allied** players' stacks can contribute. Non-allied players cannot combine stacks with one another.
- Activation is automatic when the requirements are satisfied; it does not need a separate button press.
- When two allied players supply a combination, damage credit is split **50/50** between them.
- If multiple combinations compete for the same stacks, the first eligible pair to complete resolves first. Truly simultaneous event ordering has not been finalized.
