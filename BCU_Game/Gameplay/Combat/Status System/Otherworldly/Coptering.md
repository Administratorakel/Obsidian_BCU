
**Status:** Partially confirmed — balancing details remain open.

**Related:** [[Coptering Elite]], [[EXP]], [[Boracle]], [[Boracle's Existential Crisis]]

## Definition

**Coptering is a state of confusion in which knowledge is lost.**

It is not simply uncertainty about something previously unknown.

Coptering involves losing access to knowledge that was previously possessed.

The severity and consequences may vary.

Coptering is intended to appear in gameplay before the player encounters Boracle's future storyline.

This allows players to experience the condition themselves before witnessing its effects on Boracle.

## Player Coptering

Certain enemies, particularly the [[Coptering Elite]], can inflict Coptering on players.

### EXP Loss

When a player dies while affected by Coptering, they lose:

**5% of their currently accumulated EXP progress toward the next level.**

Formula:

`EXP Lost = Current Level EXP Progress × 0.05`

Example:

|Current EXP Progress|EXP Lost|Remaining Progress|
|---|---|---|
|80%|4%|76%|
|50%|2.5%|47.5%|
|20%|1%|19%|
|5%|0.25%|4.75%|

The calculation uses the EXP already accumulated within the player's current level.

It does not use lifetime EXP or the full EXP requirement for the next level.

Coptering cannot reduce the player's level.

### Knowledge Theft

When the player loses EXP to a Coptering Elite:

- The elite steals the lost EXP.
    
- The stolen EXP represents knowledge taken from the player.
    
- The elite uses accumulated knowledge to become stronger.
    
- Stolen knowledge contributes toward unlocking additional abilities.
    

### Recovering Knowledge

The player can recover stolen EXP by defeating the elite responsible.

Other players can assist in defeating the enemy.

If another player defeats the elite:

- The original victim does not automatically lose the opportunity to recover their EXP.
    
- They can return to the location where the elite died.
    
- Their stolen knowledge remains recoverable for a limited period.
    
- Once that period expires, the knowledge vanishes.
    

The exact recovery time limit has not been established.

### Permanent Knowledge Loss

The Coptering Elite may leave after accumulating victories.

If it leaves while possessing stolen EXP, that EXP becomes permanently unrecoverable.

## Coptering and Boracle

Boracle experiences Coptering during his future existential crisis.

His condition is portrayed emotionally rather than comedically.

The player should recognize the symptoms because Coptering has already appeared earlier in the game.

### Manifestations

Boracle may:

- Forget information he previously understood.
    
- Reveal his memory loss through dialogue.
    
- Become confused while attempting familiar actions.
    
- Forget how to execute an attack he successfully performed earlier.
    

One planned combat moment involves Boracle preparing to repeat an extremely dangerous attack that the player barely survived previously.

Instead of executing it, Boracle stops.

He struggles to remember what he was attempting to do.

This moment should emphasize his confusion, frustration, and vulnerability.

### Duration of Memory Loss

**Player:** EXP stolen through Coptering represents actual knowledge loss. Recovery requires reclaiming the stolen EXP.

**Boracle:** Whether his lost knowledge eventually returns remains uncertain.

## Narrative Purpose

Coptering connects gameplay with Boracle's storyline.

The player experiences knowledge loss personally before witnessing Boracle suffer from it.

The mechanic should reinforce the importance of knowledge, understanding, and Certainty within the BCU.
