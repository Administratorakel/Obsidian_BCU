Identity: Crowd control and burst windows

Hitting with Status marks [[Enemy]] with Status stacks
Stack 1 & 2: Deal small amount of Ice damage per hit
Stack 3: Deal small amount of Ice damage and slow
Stack 4 & 5: Moderate amount of Ice damage per hit
Stack 6: Freeze:
	- Target freezes for 3 seconds
	- Party gains increased Critical Chance & Damage against that frozen target
	- Additional Ice hits extend Frozen duration by +1 second once
	- When freeze expires stacks begin from zero

Interaction with:

[[Fire]]:
- Combustion on frozen targets, increase the range of Combustion
