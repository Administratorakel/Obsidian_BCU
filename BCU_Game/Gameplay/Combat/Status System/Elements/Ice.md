# Ice — Elemental Status

**Identity:** Crowd control and burst windows  
**Category:** Elemental  
**Maximum stacks:** 6  
**Base stack duration:** 5 seconds (shared timer; refreshed by a successful application)

## Stack Progression

| Stacks | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Small Ice damage per hit. |
| 3 | Small Ice damage and Slow. |
| 4–5 | Moderate Ice damage per hit. |
| 6 | **Freeze:** freezes the target for 3 seconds and grants the party increased Critical Chance and Critical Damage against that target. |

## Full-Stack Resolution

- On reaching 6 stacks, check for an eligible allied combination before applying Ice's ordinary sixth-stack effect.
- Otherwise, Freeze lasts **3 seconds**.
- Additional Ice hits extend Freeze by **1 second, once per source**.
- When Freeze ends, that source's Ice stacks restart from zero, as established in the Ice note.
- While the target is frozen, an allied Fire Combustion can trigger [[Cool Combustion]].
