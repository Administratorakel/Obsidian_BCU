Philosophie: Distort Reality and Status enhancing control



Hitting with Status marks Enemy with Status stacks
Stack 1 & 2: Deal small amount of Instability damage per hit against non-glitched Enemies
Stack 3: Deal small amount of Instability dmg against non-glitched enemies and make the enemy more vulnerable to status application
Stack 4 & 5: Moderate amount of Status damage per hit against non-glitched enemies
Stack 6: Pseudo-Glitched:
	- Target becomes more glitched with every stack until "fully" glitched with max stacks
	- When Pseudo-Glitched, enemy stays glitched for 3 seconds and has a 5 second cooldown until the enemy can gain Instability stacks again
	- Enemy gains one random "Reality changing" debuff:
		- temporarily more vulnerable to Status dmg
		- Enemy freezes in distorted animation (stunned for 2 seconds)
		- Enemy movement becomes delayed or accelerated, skips partial animation or repeats an animation
		- The enemy appears "Awakened" with stronger looking visual and altered behavior. Upon attacking the player the enemy gains a little AoE explosion upon collision.


Against the [[Player]]:

Stack 1 & 2: Deals a small amount of Instability dmg and steals a small portion of Instability with each hit
Stack 3: Deals a small amount of Instability dmg and reduce Players Stability by 20%
Stack 4 & 5: Deals a moderate amount of Instability dmg and steals a moderate amount of Stability
Stack 6: Deals a moderate amount of Instability dmg and reduces Players Stability by 50%
