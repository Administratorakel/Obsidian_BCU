Around Weapon Mastery:

Mastery rewards scale up. For example:

- Flat DMG steadily increases from 1 → 100
    
- Attack Speed steadily increases
    
- Crit Chance steadily increases
    
- etc.
    

The milestone rewards are therefore bumps in the curve, player receivers several instances where they gain feeling of “becoming stronger”



Sword example:
 **###  Sword Mastery

- Starts at 0
    
- Maximum 100
    
- Gained by actually using/hitting with Sword
    
- Mastery is retained between uses
    
- Mastery provides combat improvements
    
- Mastery can provide DMG, %, attack speed, crit, bleed, Dash improvements
    
- Meaningful milestones
    
- Lv100 gives a Sword-wielding buff
    
- Lv100 can instead be sacrificed
    
- Sacrifice resets mastery to 0
    
- Sacrifice creates Echoed Sword Essence
    
- Echoed Sword Essence is tradable
    
- Essence is used for final Sword-related ability enhancement
    
- Mastery XP/scaling formula f(x)=(Lv)² * 100
    
- Exact rewards at each mastery milestone (?)
    
- Lv100 buff (+20% DMG, +20% Attackspeed, +20% Crit/Dmg)
    
- Resetting Mastery gives 1 Echoed Sword Essence
    
- All abilities can be upgraded**

## Mastery progression
- Each weapon type has mastery levels **0–100**; level 100 is the cap.
- Mastery progresses through eligible combat kills/credit. **Provisional:** All enemy levels may count, but substantially weaker enemies contribute less.
- Formula from the uploaded image and original notes (both forms are identical):

`f(x) = 100x² + 200x + 100 = 100(x+1)²`

Here **x is the current mastery level** and `f(x)` is the **cumulative** credited-kill milestone to reach mastery `x+1`. It is **not** the additional number of kills required at each level.

| Current mastery x | Cumulative credited kills to attain next level |
|---:|---:|
| 0 | 100 → level 1 |
| 9 | 10,000 → level 10 |
| 24 | 62,500 → level 25 |
| 49 | 250,000 → level 50 |
| 74 | 562,500 → level 75 |
| 98 | 980,100 → level 99 |
| 99 | **1,000,000 → level 100** |

From level 99 to 100 is 19,900 additional credited kills if the player has exactly 980,100 already. How reduced credit from much weaker enemies rounds/counts toward these milestones remains undecided.

## Ongoing mastery rewards
- Small intermittent boosts can include damage, attack speed, critical chance, and critical damage; the **old level-by-level numbers are not locked**.
- Major reward milestones remain at **25, 50, 75, 100**; their exact values need rebalancing.
- The old Lv.75 *stance-selection reward* and Lv.100 *custom attack loop reward* in the earlier table are superseded by the book systems below.

## Stances and Stance Books
- Every weapon starts with its **Default** stance available.
- Two additional stances: **Wide** and **Focused** (Focused replaces the old 'Fast' terminology).
- Mastery unlocks the **slots/eligibility** for these stances at as-yet-undecided mastery levels.
- To use a Wide or Focused stance, the player must also acquire that **weapon-specific, tradable Stance Book**.
- Exactly **two alternative stance books per weapon type** are currently planned.

## Combo Books — provisional
- **Mastery** unlocks the capacity for more attack-loop slots.
- **Combo Books** unlock or provide attacks/techniques that occupy those slots.
- **Stance Books** change the stance/style of the attack sequence.
- Combo Books remain a separate system **for now**; acquisition, trading and exact use rules require further design.

## Mastery 100 and Echoed Essences
- The **first** time each weapon type reaches Mastery 100, the player automatically receives **one tradable `Echoed [Weapon] Essence`** without losing mastery.
- The player may **sacrifice** Mastery 100 for **one more Essence**, resetting that specific weapon type's mastery to 0.
- Repeatedly reaching and sacrificing Mastery 100 can grant **further** Essences; the automatic first-clear Essence is awarded **only once per weapon type**.
- Essences are weapon-specific resources for the last weapon-related upgrade to an ability. Exact recipes and ability-upgrade rules remain to be designed.
- `Echoed Sword Essence` is the named example.
