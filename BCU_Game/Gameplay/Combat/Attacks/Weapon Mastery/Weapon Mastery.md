## Stances and Stance Books
- Every weapon starts with its **Default** stance available.
- Two additional stances: **Wide** and **Focused** (Focused replaces the old 'Fast' terminology).
- Mastery unlocks the **slots/eligibility** for these stances at as-yet-undecided mastery levels.
- To use a Wide or Focused stance, the player must also acquire that **weapon-specific, tradable Stance Book**.
- Exactly **two alternative stance books per weapon type** are currently planned.

## Combo Books — provisional
- **Mastery** unlocks the capacity for more attack-loop slots.
- **Combo Books** unlock or provide attacks/techniques that occupy those slots.
- **Stance Books** change the stance/style of the attack sequence.
- Combo Books remain a separate system **for now**; acquisition, trading and exact use rules require further design.

## Mastery 100 and Echoed Essences
- Any time each weapon type reaches Mastery 100, the player automatically receives **one tradable [[Echoed Weapon Essence]] without losing mastery.
- The player may **sacrifice** Mastery 100 for **one more Essence**, resetting that specific weapon type's mastery to 0.
- Repeatedly reaching and sacrificing Mastery 100 can grant **further** Essences; the automatic Essence is awarded anytime the Player reaches Lv 100 with any weapon type.
- Essences are weapon-specific resources for the last weapon-related upgrade to an ability. Exact recipes and ability-upgrade rules remain to be designed.
- `Echoed Sword Essence` is the named example.
### Weapon Mastery — General Rules
- Weapon Mastery ranges from Lv 0 to 100.
- Progression is based on qualifying kills.
- Mastery bonuses apply only while wielding the corresponding weapon type.
- Sword Mastery serves as the initial template for other weapons.
- Other weapon types may have different stats, scaling, and unique passives.
- Every weapon mastery improves Stability efficiency.
- Universal dash speed and range are not rewarded through mastery.
- Every tenth level grants a significant stat milestone.
- Regular stat bonuses become progressively stronger.

### Upon reaching Sword Mastery 100

The player reaches true Sword Mastery and gets an [[Echoed Weapon Essence]] and chooses between:

Option A — Keep Mastery
- Unlocks the Weapon Mastery Lv.100 buff while wielding a weapon

Option B — Sacrifice Mastery
- Gain an additional Echoed Weapon Essence
