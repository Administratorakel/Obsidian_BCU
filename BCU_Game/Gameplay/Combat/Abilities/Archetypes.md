## Design Principle

Universal archetypes define **what an ability accomplishes**.
Weapon interpretations define **how the weapon accomplishes it**.
Different archetypes should have distinguishable functions even when their visual appearances or attack properties overlap.

Player has ~20 universal Abilities which have different interpretations for each of the [[Weapon Types]].

All Abilities will be unlockable from the beginning for 1 ability point, gained 1 per level.
- Add a point to scale the ability up
- [[Stability Fragments]] are required for evolution to next variant
- Optional [[Stability Catalyst]] for upgrade modifiers

Cooldown & casting costs will vary.
# Universal Ability Archetypes

**Status:** 20 universal archetypes. Individual weapon interpretations are conceptual until approved.

Every weapon type interprets these archetypes differently while retaining their fundamental purpose. Weapon types may additionally have Signature Abilities.


## General Ability Cancellation Rules
- Abilities can cancel normal attacks.
- Ordinary abilities cannot normally cancel other active abilities.
- Dash/Vector Break and Decisive Strike are special cancellation exceptions.
- Neither can cancel the other.
- Neither can be cancelled by the player's own actions after activation.
- Cancelling an ordinary ability before its damage-eligible point applies 50% cooldown.
- Cancelling after its damage-eligible point applies full cooldown.
- Stability already consumed is not refunded.
- Already spawned projectiles continue after cancellation.
