Player has ~20 universal [[Abilities]] which have different interpretations for each [[weapon type]] (Each weapon type will also have [[Signature abilities]])

All [[Abilities]] will be unlockable from the beginning for 1 ability point, gained 1 per level.
- Add a point to scale the ability up
- [[Stability Fragments]] are required for evolution to next variant
- Optional [[Stabilty Catalysts]] for upgrade modifiers

Cooldown & casting costs will vary.

1. [[Heavy Strike]], a powerful single hit that converts some Stability into Power.
	- Burst damage
	- Stagger enemies
	- Reward timing
	
2. [[Area Burst]], damages enemies around the player.
	- Clear groups
	- Create breathing room
3. [[Ranged Precision]], a targeted attack from distance.
	- Engage safely
	- Hit fleeing or ranged enemy
4. [[Engage]], move toward a target while attacking.
	- Close distance
	- Keep combat fluid
5. [[Cone Assault]], a forward facing area attack.
	- Reward positioning
	- Hit several enemies in front
6. [[Enhancement]], temporarily strengthens yourself or nearby allies.
	- Preparation
	- Build identity
7. [[Piercing Strike]], a focused attack that weakens enemy defenses.
	- Universal effect: applies Armor Break
8. [[BCU Game/Gameplay/Movement/Vector Break|Vector Break]], signature movement ability. Every weapon modifies it differently, while shields further enhance it through their unique [[Vector Shield Core]] properties.
9. [[Wave Projection]], send an attack that steadily travels through the battlefield
	Examples:
		- Sword slash
		- Consecutively erupting waves on ground
		- Arrow wave
		- Magical projectile
10. [[Status Infusion]], empower attacks with enhanced status buildup. Instead of applying the 6th stack instantly, it accelerates reaching the activation threshold.
11. [[Stability Barrier]], consume Stability Energy to create a protective barrier.
	- Defensive survival
	- Resource management
12. [[Earthshatter]], a powerful attack that disrupts the ground. 
	Interpretation depends on weapon:
	- Hammer splits the earth
	- Sword sends a cutting fissure
	- Magic Gauntlets erupt arcane crystal
13. [[Tactical Interrupt]], a reliable single target stun. Long cooldown.
	- Interrupt dangerous attacks
	- Strategic timing
14. [[Whirlwind]], a spinning attack. Every weapon has different spin interpretation.
	- classic, satisfying crowd-control
15. [[Elemental Manifestation]], manifest the currently equipped [[Shield Element]] through your weapon.
16. [[Stability Discharge]], consume all current Stability Energy to dramatically empower your next attack or attacks. The more [[BCU Game/Gameplay/Combat/Stability]] consumed, the stronger the effect.
	- High risk
	- High reward
17. [[Decisive Strike]], an exceptionally powerful attack with a long cooldown.
	- designed to feel like: "Now."
	- Not an ultimate, but stronger than Heavy attack. 
	- swinging a sword a thousand times is bound to give you the ability to make a decisive strike.
18. [[Pull]], reposition enemies toward yourself or another point.
	- Sh. Morningstar hooks
	- Sword drags an enemy with powerful slash
	- Hammer creates a pulling shockwave
	- Bow might use a tether arrow
	- Magic Gauntlets use Telekinesis (?)
19. [[Push]], force enemies away or reposition them.
	- crowd control
	- creating space
	- environmental interactions
	- setting up combos (?)
20. [[Domain]], create an area that changes the battlefield.
	- How does a weapon control an area?
	- Sword: "Certainty Domain" (?)
	- Hammer: Earthquake Zone
	- Bow: Arrow Rain
	- Magic Gauntlets --> Arcane Field
	- Shield --> Bastion of Stability
