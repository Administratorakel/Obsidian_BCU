### Stability Discharge
**Archetype:** Consumes the player's current Stability to dramatically empower the next attack or attack sequence. A high-risk, high-reward ability whose strength depends on Stability spent.

**Sword interpretation — Absolute Release (suggestion):** The player channels their accumulated Stability into the blade before releasing it through a massively empowered sword strike. Greater Stability expenditure produces a stronger result.
[[Stability Discharge]], consume all current Stability Energy to dramatically empower your next attack or attacks. The more [[BCU Game/Gameplay/Combat/Stability]] consumed, the stronger the effect.
