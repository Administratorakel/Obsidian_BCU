### Wave Projection
**Archetype:** Generates a damaging attack that travels through the battlefield over time. Unlike an immediate area attack, it advances through space.

**Sword interpretation — Traveling Edge (suggestion):** The sword releases a moving cutting wave that travels forward, striking enemies along its path. Its travel behavior distinguishes it from Cone Assault.

	Examples:
		- Sword slash
		- Consecutively erupting waves on ground
		- Arrow wave
		- Magical projectile
