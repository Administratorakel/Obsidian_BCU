### Whirlwind
**Archetype:** A spinning attack that damages enemies around the user. Different weapons provide their own interpretation of rotational area combat.

**Sword interpretation — Blade Cyclone (suggestion):** The player performs consecutive rotating sword cuts while maintaining controlled movement. Unlike Area Burst, Whirlwind deals damage throughout a spinning sequence rather than as one immediate burst.
