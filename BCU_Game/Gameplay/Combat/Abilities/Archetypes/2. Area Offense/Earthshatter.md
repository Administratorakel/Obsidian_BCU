### Earthshatter
**Archetype:** Disrupts the ground through a powerful attack. Each weapon interprets this ground-based disruption differently.

**Sword interpretation — Severing Fissure (suggestion):** The swordsman strikes downward, sending a cutting fracture through the ground in a straight line. Enemies standing along the fracture are damaged by the eruption. The ground-based effect distinguishes it from Wave Projection.
