### Vector Break
**Archetype:** An advanced movement ability built upon Dash, unlocked with an ability point. Consumes Stability and gains weapon-specific effects. Shield Cores may further enhance its functionality.

**Sword interpretation — Cutting Passage (suggestion):** The swordsman surges in the chosen direction with the sword extended, damaging enemies encountered along the movement path. Emphasizes damage and distance rather than the initiation of a targeted attack.

The player cannot cancel an active Vector Break. Enemy interruptions remain possible during vulnerable frames.

[[Vector Break]], signature movement ability. Every weapon modifies it differently, while shields further enhance it through their unique Vector [[Shield Core System]] properties.
