### Status Infusion
**Archetype:** Enhances the ability of attacks to build status effects on enemies, allowing status activation thresholds to be reached more quickly.

**Sword interpretation — Tempered Edge (suggestion):** The sword is temporarily imbued with greater status-inflicting potency. Successful weapon strikes build the applicable status more efficiently.
[[Status Infusion]], empower attacks with enhanced status buildup. Instead of applying the 6th stack instantly, it accelerates reaching the activation threshold.
