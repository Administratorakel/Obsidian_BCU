### Stability Discharge
**Archetype:** Consumes the player's current Stability to dramatically empower the next attack or attack sequence. A high-risk, high-reward ability whose strength depends on Stability spent. Stability consumption happens at the very end of charging.

**Sword interpretation — Absolute Release (suggestion):** The player channels their accumulated Stability into the blade before releasing it through a massively empowered sword strike. Greater Stability expenditure produces a stronger result.
Stability Discharge, consume all current Stability Energy to dramatically empower your next attack or attacks. The more [[Stability]] consumed, the stronger the effect.
	- High risk
	- High reward


[[Ability Cancellation]]: cancelling the ability before the end, does not consume this abilities Stability costs.
