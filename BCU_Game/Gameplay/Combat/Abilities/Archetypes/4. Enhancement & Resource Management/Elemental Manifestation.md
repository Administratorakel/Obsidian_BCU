### Elemental Manifestation
**Archetype:** Manifests the currently equipped Shield Element through the weapon, altering the appearance and effect of the attack.

**Sword interpretation — Elemental Blade (suggestion):** The sword channels the equipped Shield Element into a temporary elemental cutting attack. Its exact damage, status, and visual effects depend on the element.
