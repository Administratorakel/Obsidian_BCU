### Decisive Strike
**Archetype:** An exceptionally powerful, near-instant attack with a long cooldown. Stronger than Heavy Strike, but not intended to be an ultimate ability. Can interrupt ordinary abilities, but cannot be cancelled by the player once activated.

**Sword interpretation — Final Verdict (suggestion):** The swordsman performs a single extremely fast, precise cut. Minimal preparation, almost immediate impact, and a distinct recovery animation. Its identity comes from commitment, exceptional timing, and decisive damage rather than a large explosion.

Decisive Strike cannot cancel Dash or Vector Break, and those actions cannot cancel Decisive Strike.
