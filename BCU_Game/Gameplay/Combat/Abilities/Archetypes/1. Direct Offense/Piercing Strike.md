### Piercing Strike
**Archetype:** A focused attack that weakens an enemy's defenses by applying Armor Break. Primarily used to prepare a target for additional damage.

**Sword interpretation — Armor Sever (suggestion):** A precise thrust at an enemy's weak point. Deals moderate damage and applies Armor Break. The thrust's purpose is to expose the target, not to compete with Heavy Strike in raw damage.
