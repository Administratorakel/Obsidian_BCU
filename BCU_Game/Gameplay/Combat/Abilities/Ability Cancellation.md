# Ability Cancellation

## General
- Abilities can interrupt normal attacks
- Ordinary abilities cannot interrupt other active abilities
- Ability input buffering near recovery

## Exceptions
- [[Dash]]
- [[Decisive Strike]]

## Special Cancellation
- Dash and Decisive Strike can cancel:
    - Normal attacks
    - Heavy Strike
    - Stability Discharge
    - Other ordinary abilities
- Dash and Decisive Strike cannot cancel each other
- Dash cannot be canceled once started
- Decisive Strike is committed once started

## Cooldowns
- Canceled before damage eligibility:
    - 50% cooldown
- Canceled after damage eligibility:
    - Full cooldown
- Stability already consumed is not refunded

## Projectiles
- Spawned projectiles continue after ability cancellation
