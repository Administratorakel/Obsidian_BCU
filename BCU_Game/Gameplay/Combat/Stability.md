Replaces traditional Mana/Energy system and is central resource for:
[[Abilities]]
[[Movement]]
[[Heavy Attack]]
[[Combat flow]]


Special NPC - Merchant who sets prices higher or lower, depending on current% HP and Stability.


Resource System Core definition:

- single combat resource
- shared across all abilities and actions
- [[Shield]] influences generation/usage


Stability Bar: 100 for now
Stability is gained through effectively hitting an enemy.

In combat, bar fills in ~8-12 seconds of active combat.

**

Stability Gain System base rule:

- First hit = full stability gain
    
- Subsequent hits = 5% of original value
    

**

**

Sources of Stability:

Offensive Stability:

- Basic attacks
    
- Abilities
    
- First hit per target = full value
    

  

Precision Stability

- Crits
    
- Hunter-Style probability success
    
- Marked enemies (?)
    

  

Flow Stability

- Vector Break timing
    
- Dodgingg
    
- Continuous engagement
    

  

Impact Stability

- Kills
    
- Multi-hit interactions
    
- Stagger / interrupt moments
    

  

Stability Gain Caps

Per enemy per ability:

- Limited total stability gain (~10-155 per enemy baseline)
    

Global per second cap:

- ~25 Stability /second max gain
    

Prevents:

- Aoe farming abuse
    
- Infinite loops
    
- Imbalance between builds
    

  

AoE /DoT Stability Rules

- First hit = full stability
    
- Each additional tick = 5% value
    
- Internal per-enemy cooldown for Stability gain
    

Result: AoE is strong but controlled
