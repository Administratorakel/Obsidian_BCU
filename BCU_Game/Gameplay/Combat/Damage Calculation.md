### Ph. & M. Defense

- Defense currently combines:
    - **Flat damage reduction**
    - **Percentage damage reduction**
- Higher Defense may have **diminishing returns**.
- Exact strength of diminishing returns is still undecided because the numbers need testing.

## Defense Types

The BCU currently distinguishes three Defense stats:

|Defense|Source|Purpose|
|---|---|---|
|General Defense|Shield|General defensive protection|
|Physical Defense (PH)|Attributes|Protection against physical damage|
|Magical Defense (MA)|Attributes|Protection against magical damage|

### Rules
- General Defense comes from the equipped Shield.
- General Defense is not directly increased by attributes.
- Physical Defense and Magical Defense are derived from attributes.
- The exact Defense calculations and interactions are not finalized.
- Numerical balancing is postponed until the game's foundation is complete.

### Open
