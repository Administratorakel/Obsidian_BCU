# Shield-as-Weapon System (Summary v1.1)

## Core Concept

When a Shield is equipped as a weapon, it does not replace other gear systems or globally buff the player. Instead, it acts as a Stability conversion system:

Shields turn Rift instability into defense and active combat power.

---

## 🧱 Passive Effect

- Grants +15% to +30% Defense (scales with Shield level)
    
- Pure, self-contained bonus (no interaction with other equipment stats)
    

---

## ⚡ Heavy Shield Mode (Active State)

When the player uses the Shield actively (blocking, attacking, Vector Break actions):

- Consumes Stability Energy per action
    
- Represents active “stability control” rather than passive defense
    

Examples:

- Blocking hits → energy drain per absorbed hit
    
- Shield attacks → energy cost per action
    
- Shield-based Vector Break → higher energy consumption
    

---

## 🔁 Stability Energy Loop

Energy is not just spent — it is actively managed:

### Energy is gained through:

- hitting enemies
    
- successful Vector Break usage
    
- absorbing Rift instability effectively
    

**

- Rift interaction bonuses
    

### Result:

- Aggressive play = more energy recovery
    
- Passive defense = energy depletion
    

---

## ⚖️ Design Principles

- No nerfs or stat changes to unrelated equipment
    
- Shield power stays self-contained
    
- Defense bonus is moderate, not game-breaking
    
- Active Shield play is rewarded, passive tanking is limited
    

---

## 🌌 System Identity

The Shield becomes:

A Stability Economy Controller

Not just defense, but:

- absorption
    
- conversion
    
- controlled instability usage
