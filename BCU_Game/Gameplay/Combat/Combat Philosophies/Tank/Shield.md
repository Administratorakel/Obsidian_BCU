# Shield — Stability Mechanics

## Blocking

When the player equips a Shield as a weapon, holding the normal attack button raises the Shield to block incoming damage.

### Stability Consumption

- Blocking an incoming hit consumes Stability.
    
- Stability consumption scales with the incoming damage relative to the player's maximum HP.
    
- The minimum Stability cost per blocked hit is **1%**.
    

**Current formula:**

`Stability Cost (%) = max(1%, Incoming Damage / Max HP × 100%)`

### Example

|Incoming damage (% Max HP)|Stability consumed|
|---|---|
|0.5%|1%|
|3%|3%|
|8%|8%|
|15%|15%|

## Not Blocking

Taking damage without blocking generates Stability.

### Stability Generation

- Stability gained scales with the damage received relative to maximum HP.
    
- The damage percentage is multiplied by a balancing factor.
    
- The minimum Stability reward is **1%**.
    
- The exact multiplier remains undecided.
    

**Provisional formula:**

`Stability Gain (%) = max(1%, (Damage Taken / Max HP × 100%) × X)`

**X** is an undecided balancing multiplier.

## Design Identity

Blocking prioritizes protection at the cost of Stability.

Taking damage without blocking sacrifices HP in exchange for Stability generation.

This creates a risk-versus-reward interaction between defensive and offensive resource management.
