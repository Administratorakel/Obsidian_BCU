[[Swordmaster]]




**Status:** Core identity established; balancing pending  
**Combat Identity:** Adaptability  
**Damage Type:** Physical / Magical

Weapon Identity

The One-Handed Sword is a balanced, beginner-friendly weapon that rewards combining abilities with normal attacks.

Its defining mechanic, **Sword Adaption**, allows abilities to temporarily grant characteristics associated with the three attack stances.

The weapon should be:
- Easy to learn, with additional depth through ability combinations.
- Versatile in single-target and AoE combat.
- Effective without requiring mastery of its special mechanic.
- Capable of adapting to different combat situations without switching stances.

**Design philosophy:** Weapon Mastery improves the sword's performance, while Sword Adaption improves its versatility.

# Sword Adaption

Using an ability grants a temporary stance-related bonus to the next normal attack loop.

|Ability Group| Stance Bonus    |Effect|
|---|---|---|
|AoE| Wide Stance     |Increased attack coverage|
|Offensive| Focused Stance  |Increased attack speed|
|Utility| Standard Stance |Increased resistance to interruption|

### Rules

1. Abilities cancel ongoing normal attacks.
2. After an ability, the next normal attack always begins a **fresh attack loop**, starting with Attack 1.
3. Sword Adaption applies to that entire attack loop.
4. The bonus disappears immediately when the loop finishes or is interrupted.
5. The equipped stance remains unchanged.
6. Matching bonuses enhance an already equipped stance.
7. Sword Adaption introduces no additional damage or attack-speed penalties.
8. Each ability grants only one Sword Adaption bonus.

### Stance Interactions

**Standard Stance**
- Receives additional attack coverage from AoE abilities.
- Receives additional attack speed from offensive abilities.
- Gains further interruption resistance from utility abilities.

**Wide Stance**
- AoE abilities further increase its existing attack coverage.
- Offensive abilities provide attack speed without removing its Wide Stance advantages.
- Utility abilities increase interruption resistance.

**Focused Stance**

- AoE abilities increase attack coverage without sacrificing its existing speed.
- Offensive abilities further enhance attack speed.
- Utility abilities increase interruption resistance.

Normal Attacks
- Three available stances: Standard, Wide and Focused
- Stance Books unlock alternative normal attack combinations.
- Sword Adaption modifies the next attack loop without replacing its animations or equipped stance.
- Standard Stance remains viable throughout the game.

Vector Break / Bulletjump

**Established characteristics:**
- Increased damage.
- Increased travel distance.

Further interactions with Sword Adaption are undecided.

Weapon Mastery

Weapon Mastery provides permanent progression bonuses, including damage, critical chance, critical damage and attack speed.

Sword Adaption should remain mechanically distinct by emphasizing temporary combat behavior rather than additional numerical damage bonuses.

Unresolved Questions

- Exact attack-coverage bonus from Wide Adaption.
- Exact attack-speed bonus from Focused Adaption.
- Strength and mechanics of Standard Adaption's interruption resistance.
- How enemy interruption strength and player resistance will be calculated.
- Classification of individual abilities into the three Adaption groups.
- Exact behavior when another ability is used before the enhanced attack loop begins.

**Note:** Standard Adaption's interruption resistance is provisional until enemy interruption mechanics receive numerical definitions.

---
## Core Design Principle

**The One-Handed Sword adapts to combat through its abilities without requiring the player to change stances.**

Its simplicity makes it suitable as a first weapon, while its interactions with Stance Books offer additional depth for experienced players.




### Sword Momentum Mastery Buff at lv 50/100


**Lv 50 — Sword Momentum I**

Consecutive successful hits build attack speed stacks.

- Missing an attack does not reset stacks.
- Stacks expire after a period without successful hits.
- Exact stack values and duration remain undecided.

**Lv 100 — Sword Momentum II**

Upgrades the existing passive.

- Consecutive hits increase attack speed and damage.
- Attack-speed bonuses become stronger.
- Stack expiration duration increases.
