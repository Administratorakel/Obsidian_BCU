[[Berserker]]

Weapon Mastery Progress Lv 100 bonus: Increased Stability gain from heavy attacks
**Identity:** Momentum and commitment

- Extremely slow but devastating attacks.
- Consecutive normal attack inputs generate momentum.
- Attacks do not need to hit enemies to build momentum.
- Players can deliberately charge up through repeated swings.
- Momentum increases damage, not attack speed.
- Momentum immediately resets upon interruption or consumption.

**Proposed ability interactions:**

- Heavy Attack consumes momentum for massive damage.
- General AoE preserves momentum.
- Gap Closer converts momentum into movement.
- Buff interacts with momentum preservation.
- Decisive Strike consumes momentum for a finishing attack.

Distinct from Hammer (immediate impact) and Odachi (precise counterattacking).
