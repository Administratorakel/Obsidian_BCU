# Dual Guns — Weapon Identity

**Status:** Core identity established; Weapon Mastery bonuses pending  
**Combat Philosophy:** [[Skirmisher]]  
**Damage Type:** Magical  
**Combat Identity:** Mobile Firepower and Sustained Offensive Pressure

---

## 1. Core Weapon Identity

The Dual Guns are a medium-range weapon specializing in continuous offensive pressure while maintaining unrestricted movement during normal attacks.

Unlike other weapons, the Dual Guns allow the player to move freely while automatically attacking a selected enemy.

The weapon emphasizes:

- Unrestricted movement during normal attacks.
- Rapid, continuous projectile-based attacks.
- Maintaining distance from enemies through active movement.
- Relatively low individual normal attack damage.
- Sustained damage through high attack uptime.
- Generating stacks through successful projectile hits.
- Increased stack generation while moving.
- Consuming accumulated stacks to enhance abilities.
- Defensive repositioning through Vector Break.

**Design Philosophy:** The Dual Guns reward maintaining offensive pressure while actively avoiding incoming attacks through movement and positioning.

---

## 2. Normal Attacks

### Movement During Attacks

The player can move at their normal movement speed while performing normal attacks.

- Movement does not interrupt the normal attack combination.
- Normal attacks do not reduce movement speed.
- The player can continuously attack a selected enemy while moving.
- Individual abilities may restrict movement, reduce movement speed or require the player to stand still temporarily.

### Attack Range

- Medium-range weapon.
- Normal attack range is slightly greater than the Whip's attack range.
- All three stances have approximately the same attack range.
- Exact attack distances remain undecided.

Normal attacks represent rapid shooting rather than deliberate precision aiming.

### Projectile Behavior

- Normal attacks use individual projectiles.
- Projectiles do not pierce enemies.
- Each projectile damages only the first enemy it encounters.
- Selecting an enemy behind other enemies does not allow projectiles to bypass those enemies.
- Shotgun-like projectile bursts are reserved for potential abilities rather than normal attacks.
- Selected abilities may use hitscan instead of conventional projectiles.

**Ranged Precision** is a potential candidate for hitscan mechanics.

## 3. Attack Stances

The Dual Guns support three attack stances:

- Standard
- Wide
- Focused

All three stances have approximately the same:

- Attack speed.
- Number of projectiles.
- Maximum attack range.
- Potential stack generation.

Their primary differences are firing angles, projectile distribution and attack patterns.

### Standard Stance — Balanced Fire

- Primarily straightforward shooting patterns.
- Some attacks may feature a slight spread.
- Balanced between concentrated and widespread attacks.
- Suitable for individual enemies and smaller groups.

### Wide Stance — Sweeping Fire

- Individual projectiles are distributed across wider firing angles.
- Attack patterns may resemble cones or sweeping areas.
- Certain attacks within the combination may cover up to 180 degrees.
- Does not inherently fire more projectiles than the other stances.
- Does not use shotgun-like bursts during normal attacks.

The player can learn the attack combination and deliberately reposition before particularly wide attacks to hit more enemies.

### Focused Stance — Concentrated Fire

- Narrower firing patterns.
- Primarily concentrates projectiles toward individual enemies.
- More suitable for sustained single-target combat.
- Does not inherently possess greater attack range or attack speed.

Exact attack animations, firing patterns and numerical differences will be established during weapon development.

## 4. Stack Mechanic — Name Pending

The Dual Guns possess a weapon-specific stacking mechanic that rewards successful projectile hits and mobile combat.

Accumulated stacks are consumed to enhance abilities.

### Stack Generation

Each successful normal attack projectile generates stacks.

|Player State|Stacks per Successful Projectile|
|---|--:|
|Standing still|1|
|Moving|2|
|Projectile misses|0|

The movement multiplier of ×2 is provisional.

### General Rules

- Projectiles must successfully hit an enemy to generate stacks.
- Simply firing projectiles does not generate stacks.
- Movement alone does not generate stacks.
- Standing still and successfully attacking generates stacks normally.
- Moving while successfully attacking increases stack generation.
- Each projectile is evaluated individually.

### Maximum Stacks

The player can accumulate more than 100 stacks.

- 100 stacks represent the maximum enhancement potential of an individual ability rather than the maximum number of stacks the player can hold.
- Excess stacks remain available for subsequent abilities.
- The overall maximum stack capacity remains undecided.

### Stack Expiration

Stacks reward continuous movement and gradually decay when the player remains stationary.

- Standing still begins a two-second countdown.
- After two seconds, accumulated stacks begin decreasing gradually.
- Stacks do not disappear simultaneously.
- Movement prevents stationary stack decay.
- Casting abilities interrupts the expiration countdown.
- Vector Break resets the two-second countdown.

The exact decay rate remains undecided.

Whether ordinary abilities pause or fully reset the countdown also remains undecided.

---

## 5. Ability Enhancement

Individual compatible abilities can consume up to **100 stacks** to reach their full enhancement potential.

### Example

A player possesses 250 stacks.

- First fully enhanced ability consumes 100 stacks.
- Second fully enhanced ability consumes 100 stacks.
- The player retains 50 stacks.

Abilities can receive partial enhancements when fewer than 100 stacks are available.

The exact scaling between consumed stacks and ability effectiveness remains undecided.

### Ability Category Interactions

Different ability categories may convert accumulated stacks into different effects.

|Ability Category|Proposed Enhancement|
|---|---|
|Direct Offense|Increased number of projectiles fired|
|Area Offense|Increased number of affected enemies|
|Mobility|Temporary movement-speed enhancements|
|Utility|Increased duration or effectiveness|

**These category-specific effects are proposals, not finalized mechanics.**

Increasing the number of projectiles rather than directly increasing damage could reinforce the Dual Guns' rapid-fire identity and distinguish their stack mechanic from the Adept Scythe's [[Adept Scythe - Reaping Mastery|Reaping]].

Individual ability interactions will be determined during ability development.

## 6. Vector Break — Blinking Step

The Dual Guns' [[Vector Break]] specializes in defensive repositioning rather than offensive damage.

### Established Characteristics

- Extremely fast movement resembling a blinking step.
- No offensive damage.
- No projectiles are fired during Vector Break.
- Extended invulnerability compared with standard Vector Breaks.
- Resets the two-second stack decay countdown.
- Does not consume accumulated weapon-specific stacks.
- Preserves the current normal attack combination.

Actual teleportation is not required. The movement should primarily feel exceptionally fast.

Exact travel distance, movement speed and invulnerability duration remain undecided.

### Normal Attack Continuity

Vector Break temporarily pauses the normal attack combination.

After Vector Break finishes:

- The player resumes the existing normal attack combination.
- The combination does not restart from its first attack.
- The player can use Vector Break to reposition before executing a particular upcoming attack.

This privilege is **exclusive to Vector Break**.

### Normal Dash Comparison

|Characteristic|Normal Dash|Dual Guns Vector Break|
|---|---|---|
|Movement|Standard dash movement|Blink-like movement|
|Normal attack combination|Interrupted|Preserved|
|Stack countdown reset|No special effect established|Yes|
|Invulnerability|General Dash rules|Extended Vector Break invulnerability|
|Offensive damage|None|None|

### Combat Example

1. The player performs several Wide Stance normal attacks.
2. The player recognizes that the next attack has particularly wide coverage.
3. The player uses Vector Break to reposition closer to a group.
4. The normal attack combination resumes.
5. The player executes the wide-angle attack.
6. The player uses another available Vector Break to regain distance.
7. Normal attacks continue.

This allows experienced players to incorporate knowledge of their attack combinations into their positioning decisions.

## 7. Weapon Mastery

**Status: Undecided**

The unique Weapon Mastery bonuses at Levels 50 and 100 have not yet been finalized.

### Concept A — Improved Stack Generation

- Level 50: Increase the moving stack generation multiplier from ×2 to ×3.
- Level 100: Increase the multiplier from ×3 to ×4.

This improves the existing mechanic without introducing additional complexity.

**Status:** Proposed, not selected.

### Concept B — Homing Projectiles

- Level 50: Slight projectile trajectory correction.
- Level 100: Stronger projectile trajectory correction.

This would improve successful hit frequency and therefore stack generation.

**Status:** Not currently favored due to concerns about how homing projectiles would feel and behave.

### Concept C — Stack Regeneration

- Level 50: Successful abilities refund a portion of their consumed stacks.
- Level 100: Successful normal attacks shortly after casting an ability generate additional stacks.

This rewards alternating between normal attacks and abilities.

**Status:** Reserved as a backup concept (Plan B).

### Concept D — Crossfire

A mechanic that rewards interactions between projectiles fired by both guns.

**Level 50:**

- Projectiles from both guns hitting the same enemy within a short time window trigger an additional damage instance.

**Level 100:**

- The interaction also becomes possible when the two guns hit different enemies.
- Small shockwaves appear at the affected enemies' locations.

This could create additional interactions with Focused and Wide Stance without directly modifying projectile trajectories or stack generation.

**Status:** Latest proposal; awaiting further discussion.

## 8. Distinction From Other Weapons

|Weapon|Defining Mechanic|
|---|---|
|Dual Guns|Unrestricted movement during normal attacks and movement-enhanced stack generation|
|Bow|Stationary precision attacks that generate movement-speed bonuses|
|Twin Daggers|Successful strikes generate Momentum for ability-based engagement and disengagement|
|Adept Scythe|Directional attack enhancements and Stability-based movement|
|Warfans|Stationary magical residues that enhance ability charging and effects|
|Whip|Orbital movement, enemy gathering and sustained battlefield control|
|Fighting Slab|Repeated swings generate offensive momentum|

### Dual Guns vs. Bow

The Bow rewards successful attacks with increased movement speed.

The Dual Guns reward movement during successful attacks with increased stack generation.

The Bow separates attacking and repositioning, whereas the Dual Guns allow both simultaneously.

### Dual Guns vs. Twin Daggers

The Twin Daggers specialize in rapid, ability-based engagement and disengagement.

The Dual Guns specialize in unrestricted movement while attacking and defensive repositioning through Vector Break.

**Related Design Revision:** Blink-like movement is now intended for the Dual Guns' Vector Break rather than the Twin Daggers' higher-mastery movement. Twin Daggers should retain extremely fast but visibly continuous movement.

## 9. Intended Combat Rhythm

**Move and Shoot → Accumulate Stacks → Reposition → Enhance Abilities → Continue Shooting**

The Dual Guns reward players who:

- Maintain appropriate attack distance.
- Avoid incoming attacks through movement.
- Understand their normal attack patterns.
- Position themselves to maximize successful projectile hits.
- Use Vector Break to preserve attack combinations and accumulated stacks.
- Spend accumulated stacks at appropriate moments.

Their effectiveness comes from sustained offensive uptime rather than overwhelming individual attacks.

## 11. Final Weapon Identity

**The Dual Guns are a mobile, medium-range weapon that transforms successful attacks and continuous movement into accumulated power for enhanced abilities.**

Their defining strengths are unrestricted movement during normal attacks, sustained offensive pressure and defensive repositioning through a specialized Vector Break.

Unlike weapons that commit the player to stationary attacks or predefined movement patterns, the Dual Guns allow the player to control movement independently of their normal attack combination.

**Design Status: Core Identity Established**
