## 11. Glaive / Chakram

**Identity:** Melee/ranged hybrid and precision timing

- One combined weapon category.
    
- Normal attacks are melee.
    
- Some abilities throw the weapon at range.
    
- No normal attacks while the weapon is airborne.
    
- Heavy Attack concept involves throwing the weapon and potentially causing an explosion.
    

**Precision-release system:**

- Holding abilities activates a shrinking-circle indicator.
    
- A thicker circle marks the ideal release window.
    
- Indicator glows at perfect timing.
    
- Early release performs the normal ability.
    
- Perfect timing empowers the ability.
    
- Late release still casts the ability, but with a delay.
    
- Intended to apply to all abilities initially; exceptions possible.
    
- Movement during charging is undecided.
    

**Bulletjump:** Not finalized.
