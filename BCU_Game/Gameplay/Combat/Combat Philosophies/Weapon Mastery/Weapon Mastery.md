Around Weapon Mastery:

Mastery rewards scale up. For example:

- Flat DMG steadily increases from 1 → 100
    
- Attack Speed steadily increases
    
- Crit Chance steadily increases
    
- etc.
    

The milestone rewards are therefore bumps in the curve, player receivers several instances where they gain feeling of “becoming stronger”



Sword example:
 **###  Sword Mastery

- Starts at 0
    
- Maximum 100
    
- Gained by actually using/hitting with Sword
    
- Mastery is retained between uses
    
- Mastery provides combat improvements
    
- Mastery can provide DMG, %, attack speed, crit, bleed, Dash improvements
    
- Meaningful milestones
    
- Lv100 gives a Sword-wielding buff
    
- Lv100 can instead be sacrificed
    
- Sacrifice resets mastery to 0
    
- Sacrifice creates Echoed Sword Essence
    
- Echoed Sword Essence is tradable
    
- Essence is used for final Sword-related ability enhancement
    
- Mastery XP/scaling formula f(x)=(Lv)² * 100
    
- Exact rewards at each mastery milestone (?)
    
- Lv100 buff (+20% DMG, +20% Attackspeed, +20% Crit/Dmg)
    
- Resetting Mastery gives 1 Echoed Sword Essence
    
- All abilities can be upgraded**
