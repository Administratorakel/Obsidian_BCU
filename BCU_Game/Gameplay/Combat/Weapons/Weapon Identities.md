# 2. Two-Handed Sword

**Identity:** Cleaving

- Slower but more powerful attacks than the 1H Sword.
- Broad attack arcs and strong multi-target damage.
- Emphasizes reach, positioning and deliberate strikes.
- Should feel powerful without relying on the Fighting Slab's momentum mechanic.

**Bulletjump:** Greater damage and attack range.

## 3. Hammer

**Identity:** Impact

- Heavy, forceful physical attacks.
    
- Powerful knockback and shockwaves.
    
- Strong crowd disruption.
    
- Focuses on immediate impact rather than continuous momentum.
    
- One of the slowest conventional weapons.
    

**Bulletjump:** Greater damage, shorter distance and a shockwave.

## 4. Twin Daggers

**Identity:** Engagement and disengagement

- Rapid attacks and aggressive combinations.
    
- More opportunities to approach, evade and escape than most weapons.
    
- Decisive Strike retreats backward, potentially attacking during the retreat.
    
- No freely selected retreat direction for Decisive Strike.
    
- Relies on mobility and positioning rather than heavy individual strikes.
    

**Bulletjump:** Increased damage and speed.

## 5. Shooting Morningstar

**Identity:** Chain manipulation and enemy repositioning

- Physical and magical damage.
    
- Extended attacks, pulling and unusual attack angles.
    
- Manipulates the chain and ball independently during abilities.
    

**Pull ability concept:**

1. Shoot the Morningstar overhead onto the ground at a distance, with spikes retracted.
    
2. Ground impact knocks enemies into the air.
    
3. Ball rebounds upward.
    
4. Timed chain-whipping animation raises it to enemy height.
    
5. Spikes extend and a larger spiked energy sphere appears.
    
6. Chain retracts, pulling affected enemies toward the player.
    

**Bulletjump:** Increased damage, pulling effect and jumping behind the enemy.

## 6. Magic Gauntlets

**Identity:** Magical force manipulation

- Full-hand gloves with open palms.
    
- Classified as melee weapons despite magical attacks.
    
- Palm strikes push and displace enemies.
    
- Magical force walls manipulate enemy positioning.
    
- Force walls disappear immediately after striking.
    
- Potential abilities involve collision, compression and displacement.
    

**Bulletjump:** Increased damage and pushing enemies along the jump.

## 7. Bow

**Identity:** Precision

- Physical and magical ranged damage.
    
- Strong positional play and aimed attacks.
    
- Potential emphasis on powerful shots and tactical mobility.
    
- Exact defining mechanic still undecided.
    

**Bulletjump:** Increased speed and substantially increased distance.

## 8. Warfans

**Identity:** Enhancement and magical battlefield control

- Magical attacks using wind-like effects.
    
- Wide-area attacks and buffs.
    
- Elegant, sweeping movements.
    
- Potential support-oriented ability interactions.
    
- Exact defining mechanic still undecided.
    

**Bulletjump:** Increased speed and stronger damage buff.

## 9. Dual Guns

**Identity:** Mobile firepower

- Magical ranged damage.
    
- Rapid attacks and sustained pressure.
    
- Many attacks and abilities may work while moving.
    
- Attacks may target a selected enemy or a chosen direction.
    
- Automatic fire at multiple enemies may become a specific ability.
    

**Bulletjump:** Greater speed and increased fire rate.

## 10. Whip

**Identity:** Stability tether and orbital crowd control

- Extended melee reach and sweeping attacks.
    
- Uses a connector stone at its tip to generate a Stability rope.
    

**Vector Break connection:**

- Player must Vector Break through an enemy.
    
- Contact in the first half of VB creates a larger orbit radius.
    
- Contact in the second half creates a smaller orbit radius.
    
- Player waits at the corresponding distance for a short preparation period.
    
- Left/right input initiates orbital movement.
    

**Swinging:**

- Connected enemy becomes the anchor.
    
- Rope continuously squeezes affected enemies.
    
- Additional enemies are automatically entangled.
    
- More enemies increase potential damage and Stability consumption.
    
- Swinging continuously drains Stability.
    
- Some abilities can be cast during swinging.
    
- Reversing orbit direction consumes one VB stack.
    

**Disengagement:**

- Certain abilities release the tether and launch the player.
    
- Using VB again can release the tether without consuming another stack.
    

**Bosses:**

- Can serve as anchors.
    
- Generally receive little single-target swing damage.
    
- Groups of enemies around bosses can make swinging effective.
    
- Some bosses visibly send Stability disruptions through the rope.
    
- Reaching the connector stone interrupts the player.
    

**Bulletjump:** Increased speed and greatly increased damage range.

## 11. Glaive / Chakram

**Identity:** Melee/ranged hybrid and precision timing

- One combined weapon category.
    
- Normal attacks are melee.
    
- Some abilities throw the weapon at range.
    
- No normal attacks while the weapon is airborne.
    
- Heavy Attack concept involves throwing the weapon and potentially causing an explosion.
    

**Precision-release system:**

- Holding abilities activates a shrinking-circle indicator.
    
- A thicker circle marks the ideal release window.
    
- Indicator glows at perfect timing.
    
- Early release performs the normal ability.
    
- Perfect timing empowers the ability.
    
- Late release still casts the ability, but with a delay.
    
- Intended to apply to all abilities initially; exceptions possible.
    
- Movement during charging is undecided.
    

**Bulletjump:** Not finalized.

## 12. Odachi

**Identity:** Reactive counterattacking

- Large sword with slow, deliberate normal attacks.
    
- Counterattacks may be integrated into the normal attack loop.
    
- Most abilities may parry during their opening frames.
    
- Successful parries can transition into empowered counters.
    
- Both melee and ranged attacks may be counterable.
    
- Exact projectile-counter behavior requires further design.
    
- Rewards enemy observation and timing rather than aggression.
    

**Bulletjump:** Not finalized.

## 13. Scythe

**Identity:** Continuous, supernatural combat flow

- Blade formed from Stability rather than ordinary physical material.
    
- Smooth, unnatural and dangerous movements.
    
- Normal attacks should feel like interconnected dance movements.
    
- Abilities extend or redirect the player's movement.
    
- Avoid separately designing four or five variations of every ability.
    
- Reuse shared movement animations and animation blending.
    
- Blade may extend, bend, separate or create impossible attack arcs.
    
- Reserve unique animations for selected signature abilities.
    
- Existing ability-cancellation rules still apply.
    

**Stability manipulation:** Specific additional mechanic undecided.

**Bulletjump:** Not finalized.

## 14. Shield

**Identity:** Defensive offense

- Can function as a weapon.
    
- Offense scales from defensive properties.
    
- Active blocking consumes Stability.
    
- Shield strikes, bashes and defensive abilities.
    
- Additional synergy with Vector Break and shield upgrades.
    
- Exact offensive ability identity still needs development.
    

**Bulletjump:** Not finalized.

## 15. Fighting Slab

**Identity:** Momentum and commitment

- Extremely slow but devastating attacks.
    
- Consecutive normal attack inputs generate momentum.
    
- Attacks do not need to hit enemies to build momentum.
    
- Players can deliberately charge up through repeated swings.
    
- Momentum increases damage, not attack speed.
    
- Momentum immediately resets upon interruption or consumption.
    

**Proposed ability interactions:**

- Heavy Attack consumes momentum for massive damage.
    
- General AoE preserves momentum.
    
- Gap Closer converts momentum into movement.
    
- Buff interacts with momentum preservation.
    
- Decisive Strike consumes momentum for a finishing attack.
    

Distinct from Hammer (immediate impact) and Odachi (precise counterattacking).

**Bulletjump:** Not finalized.

---

## Design Principles

- Every weapon should have a recognizable combat identity.
    
- Universal ability categories remain available, but their execution differs by weapon.
    
- A weapon's special mechanic does not necessarily need to appear in every ability.
    
- Weapons should feel different through positioning, timing, movement and resource interactions, not just damage values.
    
- Avoid unnecessary animation complexity where shared systems can produce similar results.
