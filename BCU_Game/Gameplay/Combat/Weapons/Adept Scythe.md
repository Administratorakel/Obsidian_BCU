## 13. Scythe

**Identity:** Continuous, supernatural combat flow

- Blade formed from Stability rather than ordinary physical material.
    
- Smooth, unnatural and dangerous movements.
    
- Normal attacks should feel like interconnected dance movements.
    
- Abilities extend or redirect the player's movement.
    
- Avoid separately designing four or five variations of every ability.
    
- Reuse shared movement animations and animation blending.
    
- Blade may extend, bend, separate or create impossible attack arcs.
    
- Reserve unique animations for selected signature abilities.
    
- Existing ability-cancellation rules still apply.
