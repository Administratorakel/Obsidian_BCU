Shooting Morningstar

**Identity:** Chain manipulation and enemy repositioning

- Physical and magical damage.
- Extended attacks, pulling and unusual attack angles.
- Manipulates the chain and ball independently during abilities.

**Pull ability concept:**

1. Shoot the Morningstar overhead onto the ground at a distance, with spikes retracted.
2. Ground impact knocks enemies into the air.
3. Ball rebounds upward.
4. Timed chain-whipping animation raises it to enemy height.
5. Spikes extend and a larger spiked energy sphere appears.
6. Chain retracts, pulling affected enemies toward the player.

**Bulletjump:** Increased damage, pulling effect and jumping behind the enemy.
