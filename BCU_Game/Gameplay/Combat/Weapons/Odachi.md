# Odachi — Weapon Identity

**Status:** Core identity established; mechanics and balancing pending  
**Combat Identity:** Reactive Counterattacking  
**Weapon Type:** Large, two-handed sword

## 1. Weapon Identity

The Odachi is a large, deliberate weapon specializing in counterattacks.

Its defining characteristic is the ability to **parry incoming attacks through its normal attack combinations and abilities**.

Unlike other heavy weapons, the Odachi rewards observing enemies, recognizing attack patterns, and responding with precise timing.

The weapon should:

- Feel heavy and powerful, with relatively slow normal attacks.
    
- Reward well-timed defensive actions.
    
- Turn incoming attacks into offensive opportunities.
    
- Allow experienced players to counter enemies without relying on a separate parry ability.
    
- Remain effective even when enemies do not attack frequently.
    

**Design Philosophy:** The Odachi rewards patience, observation, and precise reactions rather than continuous aggression.

## 2. Counterattack System

### Core Mechanic

The Odachi can parry incoming attacks during specific attack animations.

Potential counter opportunities include:

- Certain normal attacks within its attack loop.
    
- The opening frames of most abilities.
    
- Specialized counterattack abilities.
    

### Ability-Based Counters

Most abilities may have a short parry window during their opening frames.

**Normal execution:**

1. Player activates an ability.
    
2. No incoming attack is intercepted.
    
3. Ability executes normally.
    

**Successful counter:**

1. Player activates an ability shortly before an incoming attack connects.
    
2. The Odachi intercepts the attack during its parry window.
    
3. The ability transitions into an empowered counterattack.
    

The exact counterattack enhancements depend on the individual ability.

This system allows abilities to serve both offensive and defensive purposes.

## 3. Countering Melee and Ranged Attacks

The Odachi is intended to counter both melee and ranged attacks.

### Melee Counters

- Primary counterattack specialization.
    
- Intercepts incoming melee strikes through precise timing.
    
- Successful counters create opportunities for retaliation.
    

### Ranged Counters

- Countering ranged attacks should also be possible.
    
- The exact mechanics remain undecided.
    
- Potential interpretations include deflecting, destroying, or redirecting projectiles.
    

**Note:** Projectile reflection and redirection are possibilities, not established mechanics.

## 4. Normal Attack Combinations

The Odachi's normal attacks are relatively slow because of its size and weight.

Its attack loop may contain built-in counter opportunities.

### Proposed Characteristics

- Deliberate, powerful sword swings.
    
- Distinctive timing windows.
    
- One or more attacks capable of intercepting incoming strikes.
    
- Greater emphasis on timing than attack frequency.
    

The precise distribution of counter windows within the normal attack loop remains undecided.

### Attack Stances

The Odachi supports the general Stance Book system:

|Stance|Potential Interpretation|
|---|---|
|Standard|Balanced attack patterns and counter opportunities|
|Wide|Broader sweeping attacks and potentially wider parry coverage|
|Fast|Quicker strikes and potentially easier access to counter opportunities|

Stance-specific counter mechanics have not been finalized.

## 5. Relationship With Ability Cancellation

The Odachi follows BCU's established ability-cancellation rules.

- Abilities cancel normal attacks.
    
- Decisive Strike retains its special cancellation priority.
    
- A counter window does not automatically grant additional cancellation privileges.
    

Successful parries may alter the execution of an ability, but should not necessarily override the universal cancellation system.

## Core Design Principle

**The Odachi transforms precise defensive timing into powerful offensive opportunities.**

Its slow attacks create commitment, while its counterattack mechanics reward players who understand enemy behavior.

The player should feel capable of turning an opponent's aggression against them, rather than simply blocking attacks.
