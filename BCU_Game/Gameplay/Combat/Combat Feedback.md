# Combat Feedback

## Hitstop
- Normal attacks: 20 ms
- Heavy attacks: 40 ms
- Applied once per attack
- Triggered by first enemy hit

## Visual Feedback
- Weapon trails during active hit frames
- Small sparks on impact
- First impact produces a larger spark
- Damage numbers appear near impact
- Damage numbers drift and fade

## Enemy Reactions
- Normal damage does not interrupt attacks
- Small knockback does not interrupt attacks
- Stun interrupts attacks
- Knockback varies by weapon
