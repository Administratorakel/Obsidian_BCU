Party / Multiplayer combat

Party size: currently planned 8

Party Members: 

- Share status interactions
    
- Can interact with completed 6-stack effects
    
- Do not merge stacks
    

Important rule:

- Players apply their own status stacks
    
- Allies cannot interrupt another player’s 1-5 stacks
    
- 6 stack interaction belongs to the triggering player
