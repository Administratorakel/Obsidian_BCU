# Multiplayer — Hybrid World Design

The BCU uses a hybrid multiplayer structure.

Players can encounter one another within shared map instances, while certain activities use private instances.

|Content|Instance behavior|
|---|---|
|Normal open-world enemies|Shared within the map instance|
|Stability Rifts|Shared within the map instance|
|Personal quests|Shared or private, depending on quest|
|Party encounters|Private encounters are shared among party members|
|Dungeons|Private to player or party|
|World events|Shared|

### Stability Rifts

Stability Rifts are public encounters within their map instance.

Multiple players can contribute toward defeating the same Rift.

Rewards use the previously designed participation-based distribution system.

The details of that formula are maintained separately.
