## 6. Natural Magic and Player Magic

### Fundamental distinction

- NPCs native to the world may naturally possess magical abilities.
- Player characters use magic through **vessels** rather than developing the same innate magical capability.
- Natural magical ability is therefore not exclusive to the player or to combat-oriented characters.

### Magical development

- Magic affinity develops through environmental exposure.
- Exposure alone generally requires a lifetime to produce substantial changes.
- Children of parents who experienced prolonged magical exposure may inherit greater magical predisposition.
- The process can continue across generations.
- The Northwest desert inhabitants are particularly likely to develop natural magical abilities.
- Other populations can also become magically inclined through exposure.

### Insight and magical affinity

- An NPC's born INS value influences their potential for natural magical abilities.
- High INS does not necessarily mean someone has already developed magic.
- Environmental exposure affects whether that potential can manifest.
- Exact attribute thresholds and mathematical relationships are undecided.

### Everyday magic

- NPCs may develop minor magical abilities used in ordinary life.
- Such abilities need not be particularly powerful.
- Magic can be integrated into daily routines rather than being restricted to combat.
- The magical environment increases the likelihood of these abilities appearing among Map 1 inhabitants.
