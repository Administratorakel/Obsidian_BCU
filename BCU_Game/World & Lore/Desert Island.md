## 7. Desert — Environmental Magic

- The Northwest segment possesses a magical environmental influence.
- Its effects extend beyond its original boundaries into neighbouring segments.
- Enemies can also be influenced by the magical environment.
- The influence can affect inhabitants from any of Map 1's populations.
- A special area capable of accelerating magical development is being considered.
- That area may become important in a future quest.

**Not decided:** Whether the magic originates from the desert terrain itself or from a particular underlying source.

## 8. Desert — Magical Water

- The desert segment contains a body of water with some magical properties.
- The water has potential health-related benefits.
- It may be used to enhance potions.
- It may have applications within the Church of Borakel.
- Some of its effects may become stronger with higher player INS.

Potential gameplay applications include INS-dependent potion enhancements and magical healing effects, but exact mechanics have not been finalized.
