## Origin of Map 1

### The Fracturing Event

**Proposed explanation — not yet finalized as canon.**

KillaQ's interference with division by zero may have caused a catastrophic disturbance across multiple maps.

Several islands experienced powerful earthquakes, resulting in portions of their land breaking away.

The original islands survived, although damaged.

The number of additional fragments that might exist elsewhere is unknown.

### Boracle's Intervention

Boracle gathered fragments from different damaged islands to prevent them from being lost.

He invited inhabitants of the affected islands to volunteer for a new settlement.

The volunteers understood that their temporary home would be a patchwork of different lands.

They knew that environments or island fragments would be combined.

### From Temporary Shelter to Permanent Settlement

The original intention was to provide temporary shelter.

Over time, the inhabitants established a functioning community.

The settlement gradually became permanent.

The central village now accommodates inhabitants originating from several different islands.

Their backgrounds influence their physical characteristics, abilities, and potentially their cultures.

These characteristics are common among their respective populations but are not exclusive to them.

## The Four Original Islands

### Southeast — Pet Island

**Environment**
- Expansive green fields.
- Numerous colourful flowers.
- An idyllic environment, particularly for dogs.

**Inhabitants**
- Exclusively pet animals.
- Animals possess varying levels of intelligence.
- Some can communicate through speech, while others cannot.
- The relationship between intelligence, INS, and speech remains undecided.

**Notable inhabitant: The Top Dog**
- Female leader of the island.
- Known by the nickname "Top Dog". Real name [[Mini]]
- Creates delicious jam potions.
- Different jam potions provide different buffs.
- Exact potion effects remain undecided.

**Connection to Map 1**
- The southeastern green segment originated from this island.
- Some pets may appear as strays within Map 1's village.
- The game's currently established companions originate from the Pet Island.

**Unconfirmed**
- The island's proper name.
- Exact rules governing animal intelligence and communication.
### Northeast — Werfimo's Island

**Environment**
- Reddish terrain.
- The northeastern segment of Map 1 originates from Werfimo's island.

**Inhabitant characteristics**

Two major physical characteristics are associated with the island's population:

1. Tall stature.
2. Bulky physique.

These characteristics are very common among the population but are not exclusive to it.

Possessing both traits together is rare.

**Known family: Smith Bros**

|Character|Appearance|
|---|---|
|Father|Exceptionally tall and bulky|
|Mother|Tall, not particularly bulky|
|Anvil Bro|Shorter and bulky|
|Skinny Bro|Tall and slender|

The father possesses the rare combination of both major physical traits.

His exceptional physical strength is already established in the brothers' background.

A future quest concerning their father is planned for further development.

The father's unusual build may have cultural or historical significance, but this has not been established.

**Unconfirmed**
- Exact inheritance rules.
- Cultural significance of the physical traits.
- Additional population characteristics.
### Southwest — Autumn Island

**Environment**
- Autumn-like landscape.
- Forested terrain with trees.

**Inhabitant characteristics**
- Generally athletic.
- Appearance resembles traditional or classic hunters.

The characteristic physique may result from genetics, lifestyle, or a combination of both.

No explanation has been finalized.

These characteristics are common but not exclusive to the island's inhabitants.

**Unconfirmed**

- Whether athleticism is primarily inherited or developed.
- Specific clothing traditions.
- Cultural practices.
- Additional physical characteristics.
### Northwest — Magical Desert Island

**Environment**
- Desert landscape.
- The environment influences magical potential.

**Magical potential**

The longer an individual resides on the island, the greater their magical potential can become.

However, individuals possess different levels of natural talent and different rates of magical development.

Consequently:
- Some inhabitants may never develop the ability to cast magic.
- Others may demonstrate exceptional magical aptitude.
- Someone who spends their entire life on the island might only become capable of casting magic in old age.
- Children born on the island generally have greater magical potential.
- Magic is common among the island's inhabitants.

**Development outside the island**

Magical development can continue after leaving the island.

Training remains important.

Time spent on the island increases potential, but does not guarantee magical ability.

**Player interaction with magic**

The player primarily uses Stability rather than conventional magic.

Conventional spellcasting is not currently planned as a player combat system.

**Possible future feature — not confirmed**

Cosmetic magical interactions could be introduced through emotes, such as performing small magic tricks.

## 3. Shared Rules for Island Populations

### Population Characteristics

Physical and other inherited characteristics associated with an island are very common among its inhabitants.

They are not exclusive.

An individual's origin influences their characteristics without completely determining their appearance, personality, or capabilities.

### Environmental Influence

Some characteristics can develop through exposure to an island's environment.

The Magical Desert Island is a confirmed example.

### Cultural Influence

Lifestyle and cultural practices may also influence inhabitants.

For the Autumn Island, the relative importance of culture and genetics remains undecided.

### Migration

Volunteers from the damaged islands relocated to Borakel's patchwork settlement.

The original islands continue to exist.

The volunteers initially expected temporary shelter, but the settlement gradually became permanent.

## 4. Physical Structure of Map 1

Map 1 consists of four distinct outer segments surrounding a central village.

|Direction|Original island|Environment|
|---|---|---|
|Southeast|Pet Island|Green fields and colourful flowers|
|Northeast|Werfimo's Island|Reddish terrain|
|Southwest|Autumn Island|Autumn forest|
|Northwest|Magical Desert Island|Desert|

The segments are physically separated by gaps.

Large, ancient-looking, weathered metal staples hold the fractured land together.

Regular paths cross above the segment boundaries independently of the staples.

Players can cross gaps directly using their dash.

Most enemies can also cross the gaps.

The eastern terrain includes an elevated cliff allowing the player to dash downward from the northeastern segment into the southeastern segment.

The central village is a separate safe-zone area.

Its final circular or oval shape remains undecided.

## 5. Possible Future Storyline — Restoration

**Status: Long-term concept, not confirmed canon.**

This storyline is being considered for an update occurring much later in BCU's development, after OMNI has been introduced.

### Premise

Boracle must leave.

Before departing, he permanently restores the fragments composing Map 1 to their original locations.

The damaged islands would regain their missing territory.

### Established Limits

The consequences for Map 1's inhabitants have not been established.

The future of the central village has not been decided.

The relationship between this event and OMNI remains undefined beyond its intended placement after OMNI's introduction.
