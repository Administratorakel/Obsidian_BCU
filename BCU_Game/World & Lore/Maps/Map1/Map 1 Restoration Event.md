## Overview

Much later in the game's storyline, Boracle dismantles the original patchwork Map 1.

This occurs during his existential crisis while he is experiencing Coptering.

Boracle believes restoring the original islands will provide him with a new purpose.

The event fundamentally changes the geography of Map 1.

## 1. The Original Patchwork Island

Map 1 consists of fragments originating from four different islands.

|Segment|Original Island|
|---|---|
|Southeast|Pet Island|
|Southwest|Autumn Island|
|Northeast|Werfimo's Island|
|Northwest|Magical Desert Island|

The fragments were assembled into a shared settlement.

The village was originally intended as temporary shelter.

Over time, it became a permanent community.

## 2. Boracle's Restoration

Boracle returns the fragments to their original islands.

He repairs those islands as though they had never been damaged.

The restoration reverses the physical damage suffered by the original islands.

However, the inhabitants have built lives and relationships on the patchwork Map 1.

The restoration therefore has consequences beyond geography.

## 3. Transportation of Inhabitants

When a segment returns to its original island, living beings standing on that segment return alongside it.

**Their destination depends on their physical location, not their place of origin.**

For example:

An inhabitant originally from Werfimo's Island who happens to be standing on the Pet Island segment will return with that segment.

They will not automatically return to Werfimo's Island.

This can result in inhabitants arriving on islands other than their original homes.

## 4. Separation of Families and Friends

The sudden restoration can separate people.

Family members and friends standing on different segments may be transported to different islands.

Some inhabitants may initially believe that loved ones have disappeared or been lost.

The player will already have established travel routes and a teleportation network between islands.

These connections may help inhabitants reunite.

However, their existence does not eliminate the immediate confusion and fear.

The exact social consequences remain undecided.

## 5. Destruction of the Village

The central village was not originally part of the four islands.

Therefore, it does not simply return to one of them.

**The village itself tears apart during the restoration.**

Most inhabitants are expected to be in the village when this happens.

The event may be frightening and chaotic.

Some inhabitants might fall as the ground breaks apart.

Whether particular characters actually fall has not been established.

## 6. Falling From Map 1

An established rule of Map 1 is:

**Living beings who fall from the island respawn at the Church of Boracle.**

This rule is widely known among the inhabitants.

Normally, falling from Map 1 does not mean permanent death.

### Uncertainty During the Restoration

As Boracle dismantles the island, inhabitants may begin questioning whether this protection still functions.

The village is breaking apart.

Boracle himself is behaving unpredictably.

Even the Church's continued existence may appear uncertain.

The inhabitants may fear that those who fall will not return.

**However, the protective rule remains functional throughout the event.**

The inhabitants are safe from permanent loss through falling.

Their fear comes from uncertainty about whether the established protection still applies.

This is especially significant because the event is caused by Boracle, the embodiment of Certainty.

## 7. Consequences

The original islands are physically restored.

However, the restoration may disrupt:

- Families.
    
- Friendships.
    
- Established communities.
    
- Homes.
    
- Daily routines.
    
- The inhabitants' sense of security.
    

The full consequences remain open for future development.

The event should not automatically be characterized as entirely beneficial or entirely harmful.

## 8. New Map 1

Following the dismantling of the original patchwork island, a new Map 1 / Borakel map will exist.

No details have been established.

Its geography, village, inhabitants, and connection to the restored islands remain undecided.

## 5. Destruction of the Village

The central village was not originally part of the four islands.

During Boracle's restoration, the village tears apart as the surrounding segments separate.

However, portions of the village are carried along with the returning island fragments.

These structures subsequently appear on the restored original islands.

The village ceases to exist as a unified settlement, but not every building or structure necessarily disappears.

Most inhabitants are expected to be in the village when this happens.

The event may be frightening and chaotic.

Some inhabitants might fall as the ground breaks apart.

Whether particular characters actually fall has not been established.

The exact distribution of village structures among the restored islands remains undecided.
