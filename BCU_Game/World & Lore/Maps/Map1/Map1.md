## 3. Map 1 — Creation and Population

- Borakel assembled Map 1 from four surplus fragments, using existing material efficiently.
- The inhabitants of those fragments were offered the choice of accompanying them.
- Approximately one or two generations of inhabitants initially came voluntarily.
- The village contains populations originating from different fragments.
- Their ancestry is reflected mainly through physical characteristics.
- Present-day villagers generally share similar clothing styles.
- Individual clothing details may reflect a character's personal history.
- Characters should not be assigned personalities or occupations solely because of their ancestry.


## 4. Map 1 — Segment Characteristics

|Segment|Environment|Original inhabitants|
|---|---|---|
|Southeast|Green landscapes and colourful flowers|Pet-like animals|
|Southwest|Autumn-like forests|Agile humanoid population|
|Northwest|Desert with a body of water|Magically influenced humanoid population|
|Northeast|Dark reddish terrain from Werfimo's island|Tall or bulky humanoid population|

### Southeast — Green Island

- Primarily inhabited by pet-like animals.
- The Top Dog is their leader.
- The Top Dog can produce jam potions, as previously described in Obsidian.
- Only a few animals from this island might appear around Map 1.
- None currently requires a significant Map 1 role.

### Southwest — Autumn Forest

- Inhabitants generally have brighter skin.
- Their skin has a subtle **golden-honey undertone**.
- Body builds tend toward lean, agile, hunter-like physiques.
- Their physical characteristics emphasize mobility, flexibility, and balance.

### Northwest — Desert

- The population is particularly influenced by environmental magic.
- Magical predisposition can develop through lifelong exposure and inheritance.
- The desert also influences nearby segments, enemies, and natural resources.
- The inhabitants' appearance remains to be established.

### Northeast — Werfimo's Island

- Inhabitants generally have darker skin with subtle reddish undertones.
- Tall and bulky physiques are the two commonly expressed inherited characteristics.
- Individuals usually express one of these traits rather than both.
- Small stature is comparatively rare and described as recessive.
- Having both tall and bulky characteristics is exceptionally rare.
