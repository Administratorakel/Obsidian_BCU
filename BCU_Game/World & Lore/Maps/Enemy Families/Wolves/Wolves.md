## General behavior

- Wolves commonly live and fight in packs.
    
- Early wolves use relatively simple behavior; later wolves become more adaptive and coordinated.
    
- In Map 1's southeast, most animals are passive, with a smaller portion showing mixed aggression.
    

### Behavioral progression

1. **Rush:** Directly approach and attack the player.
    
2. **Position:** Some wolves wait, observe, or reposition.
    
3. **Flank:** More advanced wolves circle or approach from different directions.
    
4. **React:** Surviving wolves may adjust their behavior as the encounter develops.
    

Wolves may track players by scent.
