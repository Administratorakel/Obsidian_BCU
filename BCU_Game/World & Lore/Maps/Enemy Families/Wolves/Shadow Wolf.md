## Rare Shadow Encounter

- A rare encounter introduces an apparently ordinary shadow beneath the player.
    
- While present, the shadow buffs surrounding wolves' attack speed and damage.
    
- It grows gradually over time.
    
- Killing wolves accelerates its growth; wolf kills contribute approximately 50% of the shadow's natural growth contribution.
    
- Ordinary wolves can still be killed normally, including with one-hit attacks.
    

### Elite spawning

- Once the shadow encounter is active, the elite's appearance is guaranteed.
    
- If the shadow reaches full size before the final wolf dies, the elite spawns immediately with a special attack.
    
- Otherwise, the elite spawns when the last wolf dies.
    
- Surviving wolves retain the shadow's buffs after the elite appears and gain a small Stability Barrier.
    

### Foresight interaction

- After the player obtains DracoVentus's foresight, selected special-attack danger areas become visible in advance.
    
- Before that unlock, players must recognize the attack through other cues.
## Elite escalation

Four increasingly dangerous elite tiers are planned.

|Tier|Behavior|
|---|---|
|I|Limited coordination|
|II|Coordinates surviving wolves around the player|
|III|Uses abilities and buffs regularly|
|IV|Adds wolf spawning, coordination, buffs, and debuffs|

**Elite IV is [[OMNI Killerdogge]]**, associated with the Fighting Slab weapon.
