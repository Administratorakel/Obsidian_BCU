# Boracle — Certainty

## Identity

- Represents **Certainty**.
- OMNI No. 3.
- Connected to the foundations of reality within the BCU.
- Works as a stabilizing counterpart to KillaQ's creation of variation and possibility.

## Philosophy of Certainty

**Certainty is conviction earned through understanding.**

Boracle encourages beings to understand the world and make decisions confidently.

Core principles:

- Understanding should precede conviction.
- Confidence without genuine understanding is not true Certainty.
- Pretending to be certain is rejected.
- Hesitation is not inherently a virtue.
- Individuals should develop enough understanding to choose their own paths confidently.

**Goal:** Every being should better understand the world and confidently choose their own path.

A central definition:

Certainty defines what is accepted as real after understanding.

## Relationship with KillaQ

- KillaQ introduces variation, possibility, and creative deviation.
- Boracle stabilizes possibilities into consistent, understandable structures.
- Both contribute to how the BCU develops.
- Their roles are complementary even when their perspectives differ.

## Laba kein Kaba

- Discussions between Boracle and KillaQ may generate canonical events.
- Not everything discussed automatically becomes canon.
- Some events or statements are accepted into reality, while others remain possibilities.
- The process is narrative in nature and does not automatically change gameplay mechanics.

## Future dialogue concept

A player may say something that has not yet been established as canon.

Boracle can decide **mid-sentence** whether the statement becomes accepted as reality.

This could produce dialogue where an initially uncertain statement becomes true while being spoken.

**Status:** Long-term narrative concept, not an implemented dialogue mechanic.

## Representation in the game

- Connected to Map 1, the Church, and the player's introduction to the BCU.
- Dialogue should express thoughtful conviction rather than unquestioning authority.
- Dialogue portraits can convey the character's emotional reactions.
- The exact permanent NPC placement and implementation remain open.
