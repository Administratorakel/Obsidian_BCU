## Upgrade and special resources
- **Stability Fragment:** single general fragment resource obtainable from Stability Rifts for now; no active variant list.
- **Echoed [Weapon] Essence:** tradable, weapon-specific mastery-100 reward/sacrifice resource; example `Echoed Sword Essence`.
- `[Stat Resetter]`: resets manually assigned stat points.
- `[Echoed Stat Resetter]`: rare-material-crafted reset of manual and automatic allocation.
