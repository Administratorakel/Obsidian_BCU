Always gained upon reaching Weapon Mastery Lv. 100. 
After reaching Weapon Mastery Lv. 100, the player has the option to reset the progression and gain another Echoed Weapon Essence. So the Player can always gain 2 per Weapon Progression.

Item is tradable. Usable only for the weapon type that the player got it from.

Mastery isn't just personal progression. Mastery can produce something valuable to other players.

And the name leaves room for equivalents:
- Echoed Sword Essence
- Echoed Hammer Essence
- Echoed Bow Essence
- Echoed Odachi Essence
- etc.

Using an Echoed Weapon Essence to upgrade an ability to its final stage, gives it an effect like the weapon Mastery Lv 100 Weapon trail
