Gained upon reaching a Weapon Mastery Lv 100 the first time. 

Additionally one is obtainable by resetting the Weapon Mastery Progress.

Item is tradable. Usable only for the weapon type that the player got it from.

Mastery isn't just personal progression. Mastery can produce something valuable to other players.


And the name leaves room for equivalents:

- Echoed Sword Essence
- Echoed Hammer Essence
- Echoed Bow Essence
- Echoed Odachi Essence
- etc.

Using an Echoed Weapon Essence to upgrade an ability to its final stage, gives it an effect like the weapon Mastery Lv 100 Weapon trail

**How to get**

Upon reaching Weapon Mastery 100 the player reaches true Weapon Mastery the first time , the player gets an Echoed Weapon Essence and can choose  between:

Option A — Keep Mastery
- Unlocks the Sword Mastery Lv.100 buff while wielding a sword (Bonus dmg, attackspeed, crit/dmg.

Option B — Sacrifice Mastery
- Gain an additional Echoed Weapon Essence
