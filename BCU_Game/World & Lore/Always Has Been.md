# Always Has Been

## Core Concept

- Inspired by the astronaut "Always has been" meme.
- Boracle can establish new truths or replace existing truths within the BCU.
- Once a decision is made, the newly established truth is treated as if it has always been true.
- This creates a connection between actual development decisions and the philosophy of Certainty.
- Changes can be scripted or based on real-time decisions made by the developer.

## Connection to Certainty

- Certainty is acting with conviction that is earned through understanding.
- Boracle can reconsider previously established truths when presented with new reasoning or possibilities.
- The moment of reconsideration should occasionally be visible to the player.
- Facial expressions can communicate Boracle's thought process:
    - Initial conviction.
    - Brief confusion or doubt.
    - Consideration of the new possibility.
    - Realization.
    - Renewed Certainty.
- Boracle is not incapable of being wrong. He can recognize a better truth and establish it.

## Scripted Dialogue

- During conversations with Boracle, the player may question the existence or absence of something within the BCU.
- Boracle may consider the player's reasoning and establish a new truth during the conversation itself.
- Facial expressions should help communicate the transition between the previous and newly established truth.

### Example – Dragons

**Player:** Are there dragons in the BCU?

**Boracle:** No.

**Player:** Why not?

_Boracle thinks for a moment. His facial expression changes as he considers the possibility._

**Boracle:** There are dragons in the BCU.

**Player:** Since when?

**Boracle:** Always has been.

## Real-Time Development Decisions

- Actual development decisions may be presented as Boracle establishing or replacing truths within the BCU.
- Player suggestions may influence these decisions.
- The previous truth and its replacement can be explicitly shown.
- This allows development changes to become part of the BCU's identity.

### Example – Renaming Cool Combustion

- A player suggests a better name for [[Cool Combustion]].
- The developer considers and accepts the suggestion.
- Boracle replaces the existing name with the new one.
- The change is presented to the players with the phrase "Always has been."

_This is a hypothetical example. Cool Combustion remains unchanged for now._

## Patch Notes

Patch notes are divided into two separate areas.

### Development Changes

- Ordinary balancing changes.
- Bug fixes.
- Technical improvements.
- Other conventional development changes.

### Always Has Been

- Deliberate revisions of established truths.
- Renaming of existing concepts.
- Selected lore corrections.
- Establishment of new truths where appropriate.
- Accepted community suggestions that deliberately alter established canon.

The previous truth and its replacement can be displayed alongside each other.

### Example

- ~~Previous Name~~ → New Name

_Always has been._

## Recurring Joke and Subversion

- The phrase should become recognizable to players over time.
- Experienced players may begin anticipating the phrase when Boracle visibly reconsiders something.
- Once the pattern is established, it can occasionally be deliberately broken for comedic effect.

### Example – Reversed Expectations

**Player:** Wait, has it always been like this?

**Boracle:** No, I changed it yesterday.

- Subverting the established pattern can become part of the recurring joke itself.
- The phenomenon should not become so predictable that every appearance loses its comedic value.

## Possible Presentation

- In-game dialogue.
- Character facial expressions.
- Special visual sequences inspired by the original meme.
- Real-time replacement of names or descriptions within the UI.
- Dedicated patch note entries.
- Occasional direct appearances of Boracle.

## Design Intent

- Connect real-world development with in-universe lore.
- Turn selected retcons into intentional narrative moments.
- Allow community suggestions to become part of the BCU's history.
- Reinforce the philosophy of Certainty.
- Establish a recurring BCU-specific joke.
- Build player expectations that can occasionally be subverted.
- Preserve the significance of Boracle's interventions.

## Open Questions

- How exactly should these changes be presented in-game?
- Should the player character remember the previous truth?
- Do ordinary inhabitants remember previous truths?
- Should this phenomenon have an official in-universe name?
- How frequently should Boracle intervene in this manner?
- What are the limitations of retroactively establishing truths?
