
**Status:** Planned future storyline — major narrative direction established, details unfinished.

**Related:** [[Boracle]], [[KillaQ]], [[Coptering]], [[Map1 Restoration Event]], [[Storyline]]

## Timeline

This storyline takes place much later in the game, after the OMNI Update.

It is intended to unlock additional content.

The precise quest structure and progression requirements remain undecided.

## 1. Boracle's Accomplishments

Before his existential crisis, Boracle accomplishes two important things.

### Understanding the Player's Existence

Boracle helps the player understand the symbiotic relationship between their existence and KillaQ's continuous attempts to divide by zero.

### Reuniting OMNI Uncertainties

Boracle also helps the OMNI Uncertainties find one another.

These accomplishments contribute to his later perception that his work is finished.

## 2. The Loss of Purpose

After these events, Boracle begins believing that he is no longer needed.

This belief is incorrect.

Other beings still value him.

However, Boracle increasingly perceives himself as unnecessary.

He continues trying to help, even when no assistance is required.

He feels like a fifth wheel.

His self-worth has become excessively dependent on being useful to others.

## 3. The Beginning of Uncertainty

Boracle's emotional turmoil initially causes him to create uncertainty unintentionally.

He eventually recognizes what is happening.

Rather than rejecting it, he deliberately embraces uncertainty as a possible new purpose.

The progression is:

**Unintentional uncertainty → Recognition → Deliberate uncertainty**

This does not mean Boracle becomes evil.

His behavior emerges from confusion, frustration, and his search for meaning.

## 4. Coptering

Boracle enters a state of [[Coptering]].

Coptering involves confusion and the loss of previously possessed knowledge.

His condition becomes severe during the storyline.

He experiences difficulty remembering things he previously understood.

### Emotional Presentation

These moments should be emotional rather than comedic.

Boracle should not be portrayed as foolish.

His confusion should reveal his vulnerability and frustration.

### Combat Manifestation

During his eventual boss encounter, Boracle may execute an extremely dangerous attack.

The player barely survives.

Later, Boracle attempts to repeat the attack.

However, he cannot remember what he was trying to do.

He stops, confused.

Dialogue and animation should communicate the loss.

This is intended as a tragic moment rather than a joke.

### Uncertain Recovery

Whether Boracle's forgotten knowledge eventually returns has not been established.

His condition is severe, but its permanence remains uncertain.

## 5. Restoring the Original Islands

While experiencing Coptering, Boracle comes to believe that restoring the fragments of Map 1 to their original islands will give him a new purpose.

He dismantles the patchwork island.

The original fragments return to their respective islands.

Boracle repairs the original islands as though they had never been damaged.

The detailed consequences are documented in [[Map1 Restoration Event]].

### Motivation

Boracle sees the restoration as a meaningful act.

However, his understanding of the consequences is impaired.

The restoration may physically repair the original islands while disrupting the lives of the inhabitants who built a community on Map 1.

The full consequences remain open.

## 6. Confrontation

The player eventually confronts Boracle.

At this point, Boracle is:

- Frustrated.
    
- Enraged.
    
- Deeply affected by Coptering.
    
- Struggling with his perceived lack of purpose.
    

The player fights and defeats him.

The confrontation helps bring Boracle back to his senses.

The fight is not intended to establish Boracle as evil.

## 7. Boracle's Realization

The central realization concerns the relationship between helping others and being needed by them.

**Boracle helped someone become capable of acting without his guidance.**

The player no longer depending on Boracle does not mean Boracle's existence was meaningless.

His guidance helped the player become independent.

That independence is evidence of his accomplishment, not proof of his failure.

Boracle also recognizes a deeper problem.

He has been searching for purpose almost exclusively through his usefulness to others.

Continuing to depend on being needed will repeatedly hurt him.

He needs to discover meaning for himself.

### Connection to Certainty

Boracle's philosophy states:

**Certainty is conviction earned through understanding.**

During his crisis, Boracle became convinced of his own uselessness without properly understanding how others valued him.

His eventual realization reflects the philosophy he previously taught.

## 8. Boracle's Departure

Following the confrontation, Boracle decides to leave.

He begins searching for meaning for himself.

He leaves behind an **Echo of himself**.

### The Echo

The Echo's exact nature is undecided.

Not yet established:

- Its capabilities.
- Its knowledge.
- Its gameplay role.
- Its relationship to the original Boracle.
- Whether it can communicate with the player.
- Whether it retains Boracle's responsibilities.

The Echo should not yet be treated as a replacement deity or a fully defined NPC.

## 9. The New Borakel Map

After the original Map 1 is dismantled, a new Map 1 / Borakel map will exist.

No details about this new map have been established.

Its location, structure, inhabitants, and relationship to the original village remain open.
